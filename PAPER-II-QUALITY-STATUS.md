## Important content limitations

The online HTML edition is a selected reading edition, *not* an exact mirror of all Markdown and 15 PDF files in the project archive. The full PDF collection remains outside this GitHub repository until a suitable binary synchronization method is used.

The historical sections and sample mains answers require an additional source-verification and answer-depth pass. Original practice questions must never be labelled previous-year questions. Dates, inscriptions, treaties and named monument attribution must be checked against authoritative sources.

## Reusable standard for remaining topics

Each official syllabus clause must map to its own detailed explanation, with named people, chronology, provisions of treaties/legislation, institutions, artistic monuments, inscriptions, geographic cases, data year/units and counterarguments where relevant. Mains practice must contain a clear exam-focused blueprint and **substantive professional model answers**, not generic four-line responses.

# Visual Learning — Topic 1

## Sources and interpretation

Archaeological objects → context and dating → interpretation → comparison with texts and inscriptions → provisional historical reconstruction.

**Caution:** Interpretation must never be presented as if it were directly excavated evidence.

## Political and cultural sequence (approximate)

Mature Harappan cities (c. 2600–1900 BCE) → post-urban regional developments → layered Vedic textual traditions → early historic towns and shramana movements (first millennium BCE) → Mauryan empire (4th–2nd centuries BCE) → Satavahana and other regional powers → Gupta-era cultural and political formations (4th–6th centuries CE).

**Not a single linear replacement:** regional societies and traditions overlap.

## Religious thought comparison

| Dimension | Jainism | Buddhism |
|---|---|---|
| Self | Enduring jiva | Anatta (no permanent self) |
| Karmic process | Bondage of soul | Conditioned ethical causation |
| Ethical focus | Especially stringent ahimsa and vows | Middle Way and Eightfold Path |
| Institutions | Monastic and lay communities | Sangha and lay patronage |

## Mains concept map

Evidence → Economy and ecology → Social organization → Political institutions → Religion and patronage → Art and cultural production.

Use this causal framework to avoid listing monuments without explaining the societies that produced them.

**What the Archaeological Survey of India published:** Its official tenders index lists an entry dated **16 September 2025** for **“Annual Repairs and Maintenance to Ramappa Temple, Palampet 2025-26 - Execution of Work,”** with a listed closing date of **6 October 2025**. A separate conservation-and-minor-structural-repairs entry also appears in the same index. Source: https://asi.nic.in/HQ/tenders/?p=17 (accessed in the 22 October 2026 production audit).

**Interpretation:** The record is evidence that monument-maintenance work was **tendered**, not that a contract was awarded, that repairs were finished, or that the temple's condition improved. This illustrates the continuing institutional responsibilities surrounding Kakatiya heritage and the practical distinction between UNESCO inscription, conservation planning, procurement and completed fieldwork.

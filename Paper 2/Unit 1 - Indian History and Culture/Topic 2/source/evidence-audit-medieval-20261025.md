## Open academic gaps

- The critical editions of Barani, Amir Khusrau, Abu al-Fazl, the Kabir textual traditions and original Sufi malfuzat were not retrieved and independently compared. Do not attribute direct quotations to them on the basis of this supplement.
- Complete epigraphic corpora for Kakatiya, Vijayanagara and Qutb Shahi sites were not obtained; individual UNESCO monument descriptions are not substitutes for all inscriptions.
- No independent subject-expert has reviewed the chapter's claim-level accuracy, historiography or completeness. The original published PDF remains intact.
- The 2010 Hyderabad submission is a **tentative-list entry**; never call Charminar a UNESCO-inscribed World Heritage site.

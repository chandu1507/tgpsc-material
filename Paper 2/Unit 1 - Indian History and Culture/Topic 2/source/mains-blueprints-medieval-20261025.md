4. **Discuss Kakatiya and Vijayanagara contributions to language, art and architecture.** — Background → Kakatiya contributions → Vijayanagara contributions → Conclusion. Cite one specific dated monument/text; qualify the evidence.

5. **Evaluate Delhi Sultanate and Mughal contributions to literature and fine arts.** — Background → Delhi Sultanate → Mughal developments → Limits. Cite one specific dated monument/text; qualify the evidence.

6. **Analyse composite culture in the Deccan with special reference to Hyderabad.** — Background → Regional foundations → Hyderabad example → Assessment. Cite one specific dated monument/text; qualify the evidence.

# Quick Revision — Topic 2

**Source caution:** These are revision points, not a substitute for the full discussion.

- Islamic contact: maritime commerce and migration **as well as** political conquest; do not conflate chronology.
- Delhi Sultanate: conventional starting date **1206**; major dynastic phases Mamluk, Khalji, Tughlaq, Sayyid, Lodi.
- **Iqta:** revenue/service assignment; not automatically private land ownership.
- **Bhakti:** diverse devotional traditions; Alvars, Nayanars, Virashaiva, Varkari, northern sant traditions are not identical.
- **Saguna / nirguna:** useful categories, but many traditions resist rigid classification.
- **Silsila:** spiritual lineage. **Khanqah:** Sufi centre. **Dargah:** shrine. **Dhikr:** remembrance. **Sama:** attentive devotional listening, contested in some contexts.
- **Chishti / Suhrawardi:** compare patterns of patronage and institutional relationships without stereotyping.
- **Ramappa Temple:** Kakatiya-era monument at Palampet; UNESCO World Heritage inscription **2021**.
- **Vijayanagara:** Hampi; temples, markets, water management and multilingual literary patronage.
- **Composite culture:** historical exchange and adaptation, not the disappearance of religious differences or conflict.

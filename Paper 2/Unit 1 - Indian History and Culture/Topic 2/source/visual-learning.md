## Comparison framework

| Dimension | Bhakti traditions | Sufi traditions |
|---|---|---|
| Diversity | Regional and doctrinally diverse | Multiple orders and lineages |
| Expressions | Devotional poetry, temple or community practices | Dhikr, guidance, khanqahs, some sama traditions |
| Languages | Sanskrit and many vernaculars | Arabic/Persian and regional languages |
| Institutions | Temples, communities, teachers | Silsilas, khanqahs, dargahs |
| Caution | Not universally anti-caste | Not universally detached from rulers |

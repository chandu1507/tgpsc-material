# Original practice — not official TGPSC PYQs

### MCQ 1
Which development most directly allowed the Company to fund expansion from Bengal revenues?

A. Treaty of Bassein  B. Diwani grant of 1765  C. Annexation of Punjab  D. First passenger railway

**Answer: B.** The diwani conferred revenue-collection rights in Bengal, Bihar and Orissa. A is a later Maratha-related subsidiary alliance; C occurred in 1849; D in 1853.

### MCQ 2
Which statement about Awadh in 1856 is most accurate?

A. Annexed under Doctrine of Lapse  B. Annexed after the Third Carnatic War  C. Annexed on the stated ground of misgovernment  D. Annexed after the Second Anglo-Sikh War

**Answer: C.** British authorities justified annexation by allegations of misgovernment; it was not a Doctrine of Lapse case. The other options misstate the legal basis or chronology.

### MCQ 3
Which pair correctly matches the settlement with its typical assessment unit?

A. Ryotwari–zamindar  B. Mahalwari–village estate  C. Permanent–individual ryot  D. Mahalwari–port customs

**Answer: B.** Mahalwari typically assessed a village estate or mahal. Ryotwari centered on cultivators; Permanent Settlement relied on zamindars.

# References and verification register

## Stable foundational reading (bibliographic references; chapter-level checks pending)

- Bipan Chandra, *History of Modern India* (Orient BlackSwan): colonial conquest, economic critique and nationalism.
- Sekhar Bandyopadhyay, *From Plassey to Partition and After* (Orient BlackSwan): political and social history with historiographical discussion.
- Tirthankar Roy, *The Economic History of India, 1857–1947* (Oxford University Press): economic change and regional variation.
- Irfan Habib, *Indian Economy under Early British Rule, 1757–1857* (Tulika Books): early colonial economy.
- NCERT, *Our Pasts III* and *Themes in Indian History*, relevant chapters (edition-dependent).
- TGPSC Group-I Services Notification 02/2024, Annexure-II (official syllabus; text in syllabus-index.md).

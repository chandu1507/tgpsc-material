# Visual Learning — Topic 3

## The fiscal-military loop

Trading privileges → Political alliances → Military victories → Revenue rights → Larger army and credit → Further territorial influence

## Revenue systems decision map

- **Zamindar as revenue intermediary** → Permanent Settlement (not every landlord relation was identical)
- **Individual cultivator assessed** → Ryotwari
- **Village estate or mahal assessed** → Mahalwari

## Dual-use infrastructure matrix

| Institution | Colonial state advantage | Indian social/economic use |
|---|---|---|
| Railway | Troops, exports, port links | Mobility, internal trade, nationalist circulation |
| Telegraph | Rapid command and intelligence | Faster commercial information |
| Post | Administrative coordination | Correspondence, newspapers, political association |

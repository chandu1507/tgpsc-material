# Mains answer-writing blueprints — original prompts

## Q1: 1857 (20 marks)
**Opening:** Describe a multi-centered rebellion with sepoy origins and diverse civil participation. **Body:** political annexation; military grievances and cartridge trigger; agrarian/elite participation; regional centers and limits; causes of defeat; Crown takeover and army reorganization. **Nuance:** competing labels of mutiny and first war of independence. **Conclusion:** watershed, not geographically universal revolution.

## Q2: Tribal and peasant uprisings (15 marks)
**Opening:** Link colonial property, revenue and markets to protest. **Body:** Santhal Hul, Kol, Ulgulan; Indigo and Deccan cases; compare customary land rights, coercive contracts and debt; discuss methods and repression. **Nuance:** overlapping categories and regional specificity. **Conclusion:** resistance exposes unequal market and state power.

## Q3: Anti-caste movements (20 marks)
**Opening:** Political freedom and social equality were connected but not identical goals. **Body:** Phules, Narayana Guru, Ayyankali, Pandita Ramabai; Justice Party and Periyar; Gandhi–Ambedkar debate; education, representation, gender and caste annihilation. **Nuance:** movements differed in social bases and political strategies. **Conclusion:** widened citizenship claims and contested nationalist priorities.

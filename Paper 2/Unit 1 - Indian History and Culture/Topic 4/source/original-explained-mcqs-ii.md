# Original explained practice MCQs — Topic 4

**These are newly authored practice questions, not official TGPSC PYQs.**

### MCQ 1. Which movement most clearly involved opposition to coercive indigo crop contracts in Bengal?

A. Kol uprising
B. Indigo revolt
C. Ulgulan
D. Poona Pact

**Correct answer: B.** The Indigo revolt of 1859–60 centred on planter pressure and cultivation contracts; the others concerned different regions or political questions.

### MCQ 2. Which official archival commission is explicitly catalogued for the 1875 Deccan disturbances?

A. Indigo Commission, 1860
B. Deccan Riots Commission, 1876
C. Simon Commission, 1928
D. Hunter Education Commission, 1882

**Correct answer: B.** The National Archives of India lists the 1876 Deccan Riots Commission. A commission catalogue is not equivalent to reading every testimony.

### MCQ 3. Which pairing correctly distinguishes two different contexts?

A. Awadh—Doctrine of Lapse; Jhansi—misgovernment
B. Awadh—misgovernment; Jhansi—Doctrine of Lapse dispute
C. Awadh—Permanent Settlement; Jhansi—Indigo contract
D. Awadh—Poona Pact; Jhansi—Aligarh

**Correct answer: B.** The Company annexed Awadh in 1856 on the stated ground of misgovernment; the Doctrine of Lapse was relevant to Jhansi.

### MCQ 4. What is the strongest interpretation of the cartridge controversy in 1857?

A. Only cause of revolt
B. Immediate trigger amid accumulated grievances
C. Unrelated to sepoys
D. Cause of Congress formation

**Correct answer: B.** Cartridge anxieties sharpened distrust but cannot explain annexation grievances, landed participation or regional variation.

### MCQ 5. What changed institutionally under the Government of India Act 1858?

A. Congress gained power
B. Company governance transferred to Crown
C. Separate electorates introduced
D. Permanent Settlement repealed everywhere

**Correct answer: B.** The 1858 Act ended East India Company rule and transferred governing authority to the British Crown.

### MCQ 6. Why should the Queen’s Proclamation of 1858 be treated cautiously?

A. It is fictitious
B. It proves all promises were fulfilled
C. It records commitments, not universal implementation
D. It founded Arya Samaj

**Correct answer: C.** The original proclamation is catalogued in India Office Records; a promise about princely states or religion must be distinguished from later practice.

### MCQ 7. Which source type best tests a claim about an 1857 participant’s stated aims?

A. Undated modern slogan
B. Contemporary proclamation or correspondence
C. School poster alone
D. Random casualty estimate

**Correct answer: B.** Dated participant texts can illuminate stated objectives; their representativeness and authenticity still need examination.

### MCQ 8. Which factor enabled pan-regional nationalist political discussion without ensuring inclusion?

A. Print and communication networks
B. Universal adult franchise in 1885
C. End of caste hierarchy
D. A single official Indian language

**Correct answer: A.** Print, postal services and railways connected debates, but access remained unequal by literacy, caste, class and gender.

### MCQ 9. What happened in 1885?

A. Founding of Indian National Congress
B. Poona Pact
C. Self-Respect Movement founded
D. Queen’s Proclamation

**Correct answer: A.** Congress was founded in 1885, an institutional landmark rather than the beginning of all anti-colonial thought.

### MCQ 10. Which of the following is the best distinction between Brahmo and Arya approaches?

A. Brahmo rejected all education; Arya rejected Vedas
B. Brahmo stressed reformist monotheism; Arya invoked Vedic authority
C. Both founded by Sir Syed
D. Both formed in 1932

**Correct answer: B.** Brahmo institutions developed around religious reasoning and monotheism; Arya Samaj, founded in 1875, appealed to Vedic authority.

### MCQ 11. Which pair is correct?

A. Arya Samaj—Dayanand Saraswati
B. Brahmo Sabha—Periyar
C. Aligarh College—Ambedkar
D. Satyashodhak Samaj—Annie Besant

**Correct answer: A.** Dayanand founded Arya Samaj in 1875; the other organisations have different founders and histories.

### MCQ 12. Which statement about Aligarh is historically careful?

A. Sir Syed founded AMU in 1920 personally
B. MAO College began in 1875 and AMU was established in 1920
C. Aligarh rejected modern education
D. Aligarh represented every Muslim political view

**Correct answer: B.** Sir Syed’s educational initiative began with MAO College in 1875; the university was constituted later, in 1920.

### MCQ 13. Which text by Jyotirao Phule is catalogued with a publication date of 1873?

A. Annihilation of Caste
B. Gulamgiri
C. Hind Swaraj
D. Nil Darpan

**Correct answer: B.** The Library of Congress catalogues Phule’s Gulamgiri (1873), a critique of caste domination.

### MCQ 14. Which reformer should be recognised as an educator and organiser in her own right?

A. Savitribai Phule
B. Queen Victoria
C. Begum Hazrat Mahal
D. Rani Lakshmibai

**Correct answer: A.** Savitribai taught and organised educational and social initiatives; reducing her to a spouse obscures her historical agency.

### MCQ 15. The Satyashodhak Samaj is most closely associated with which agenda?

A. Colonial military recruitment
B. Anti-caste social organisation and criticism of priestly monopoly
C. Indigo export monopoly
D. Partition negotiations

**Correct answer: B.** The Samaj, established in 1873, challenged caste subordination and exclusive religious authority.

### MCQ 16. Why is Pandita Ramabai important to the history of women’s reform?

A. Only for military leadership
B. For women’s education, shelter and critique of patriarchal restrictions
C. For founding Congress
D. For writing the 1858 Act

**Correct answer: B.** Ramabai linked education and shelter to material autonomy for women; her religious and institutional choices also generated debate.

### MCQ 17. The Aruvippuram consecration is associated with:

A. Narayana Guru
B. Birsa Munda
C. Annie Besant
D. Dadabhai Naoroji

**Correct answer: A.** Narayana Guru’s 1888 consecration challenged restrictions on ritual authority in Kerala.

### MCQ 18. Ayyankali’s activism most directly challenged which exclusion?

A. Railway freight rates alone
B. Access to public roads and schooling
C. Company annexation of Awadh
D. British naval recruitment

**Correct answer: B.** Ayyankali mobilised for mobility, education and dignity among oppressed communities in Travancore.

### MCQ 19. Which organisation is associated with Ayyankali and generally dated to 1907?

A. Sadhu Jana Paripalana Sangham
B. Brahmo Sabha
C. South Indian Liberal Federation
D. Home Rule League

**Correct answer: A.** The Sadhu Jana Paripalana Sangham organised social and educational demands; the date is commonly given as 1907.

### MCQ 20. The South Indian Liberal Federation/Justice Party arose in:

A. 1916, Madras Presidency
B. 1857, Awadh
C. 1873, Pune
D. 1936, Lahore

**Correct answer: A.** The Justice Party tradition began in the Madras Presidency in 1916 amid non-Brahmin demands for representation.

### MCQ 21. What distinguishes the Self-Respect Movement associated with Periyar?

A. Only demands for indigo price
B. Challenge to caste, ritual hierarchy and patriarchal practices
C. Restoration of Company rule
D. Founding of MAO College

**Correct answer: B.** The movement associated with Periyar from 1925 addressed everyday social hierarchy as well as political access.

### MCQ 22. What is a defensible comparison between Justice Party and Self-Respect politics?

A. Identical in every decade
B. Representation-focused politics and broader social radicalism overlapped but differed
C. Neither addressed caste
D. Both started in 1857

**Correct answer: B.** Justice Party advocacy often stressed access to offices and representation; Self-Respect politics more radically questioned social and ritual power.

### MCQ 23. The Poona Pact (1932) primarily addressed:

A. Railway construction
B. Political representation of Depressed Classes
C. Deccan cotton exports
D. The 1858 proclamation

**Correct answer: B.** It replaced proposed separate electorates with reserved seats in joint electorates, amid Gandhi–Ambedkar disagreement.

### MCQ 24. What is the best source for studying Ambedkar’s 1936 argument against caste?

A. An unattributed quote graphic
B. An edited primary text of Annihilation of Caste with provenance
C. A colonial army list
D. A census map alone

**Correct answer: B.** Columbia’s study environment identifies the source edition and editorial changes to Ambedkar’s 1936 text.

### MCQ 25. Which statement about Gandhi and Ambedkar is strongest?

A. They agreed on every method
B. They differed on representation and the depth of caste reform despite overlapping opposition to untouchability
C. Both founded Brahmo Sabha
D. Their debate occurred only in 1857

**Correct answer: B.** The Poona Pact and later texts reveal disagreements about autonomous political voice, social unity and structural caste reform.

### MCQ 26. How should nineteenth-century Hyderabad State be classified?

A. A directly ruled British province identical to Bengal
B. A princely state under British paramountcy
C. A part of the Madras Presidency
D. An independent modern republic

**Correct answer: B.** Hyderabad’s Nizam ruled a princely state under paramountcy; direct provincial revenue arrangements cannot be mechanically transplanted.

### MCQ 27. Which event must not be moved into the nineteenth century?

A. Santhal Hul
B. Indigo revolt
C. Telangana armed peasant struggle of the 1940s
D. Kol uprising

**Correct answer: C.** The Telangana armed peasant struggle belongs to the twentieth century; it is not an 1857 uprising.

### MCQ 28. What can the National Archives catalogue alone confirm about the Indigo Commission?

A. That every witness spoke truth
B. That a report dated 1860 is held/catalogued
C. That all cultivators received compensation
D. That indigo vanished

**Correct answer: B.** The catalogue verifies the report’s existence and date, not the truth of every testimony or policy outcome.

### MCQ 29. Which is the best explanation for why nationalism did not automatically create equality?

A. Communications never spread
B. Political networks expanded while caste, class and gender barriers persisted
C. All Indians had equal franchise by 1885
D. There were no reform associations

**Correct answer: B.** Political integration and political inclusion are separate processes; communication can expand without equal access to power.

### MCQ 30. Which exam answer structure best fits a comparative reform question?

A. Only founders and dates
B. Setting, institutions, social base, methods, achievements, limitations and comparison
C. Only one quote
D. A list of unsourced casualty numbers

**Correct answer: B.** Analytical comparison requires institutions and consequences, not memorisation of isolated names or dates.

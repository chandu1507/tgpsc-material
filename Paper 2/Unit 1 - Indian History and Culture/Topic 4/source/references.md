# Foundational reading and source audit

- Sekhar Bandyopadhyay, *From Plassey to Partition and After* — colonial society, 1857, nationalism and reform.
- Bipan Chandra, *History of Modern India* — uprisings, nationalism and reform movements.
- Sumit Sarkar, *Modern India, 1885–1947* — nationalism and social politics.
- Rosalind O’Hanlon, *Caste, Conflict and Ideology: Mahatma Jotirao Phule and Low Caste Protest in Nineteenth-Century Western India* — Phule and caste critique.
- Gail Omvedt, *Cultural Revolt in a Colonial Society* — non-Brahmin politics.
- NCERT, *Our Pasts III*, chapters on tribal communities, 1857 and reform (edition dependent).
- TGPSC Notification 02/2024 Annexure-II — inherited official syllabus transcription.

**Audit note:** These are bibliographic leads. Specific statements have not yet been independently page-verified; official PYQs and 2026 current affairs remain unverified.

## Material still missing

Original 1920 AITUC proceedings, complete 1936 AIKS conference minutes, Hyderabad State police and revenue files, comprehensive Sangham membership registers, contemporary reports of Komaram Bheem's struggle, full original Cabinet Mission correspondence, authenticated Radcliffe boundary working papers, and certified Partition casualty registers were not retrieved. Exact casualty totals, strike totals, land redistribution figures and unverified slogans are therefore deliberately omitted.

## Review status

The historical narrative is a research draft with named sources, **not an independent expert-certified textbook**. All 75 topic approvals remain zero. A qualified historian must check source-specific claims, contradictory historiography and the complete original texts before approval.

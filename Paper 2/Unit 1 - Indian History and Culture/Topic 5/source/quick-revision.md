# Quick Revision — Flexible high-yield map

## Three-phase framework

- **1885–1905**: early Congress, petitions, representative institutions, civil rights and drain theory. The Congress first met in December 1885 under W. C. Bonnerjee.
- **1905–1919**: Bengal partition protests, Swadeshi, boycott, assertive nationalism, revolutionary networks, Home Rule and Lucknow Pact. The periodization is a teaching aid, not a hard boundary.
- **1919–1947**: Rowlatt protests; Non-Cooperation (1920–22); Civil Disobedience (from 1930); Quit India (1942); wartime politics, negotiated transfer and Partition (1947).

## Precision dates

- 1905: partition of Bengal and launch of Swadeshi mobilization.
- 1907: Surat split.
- 1916: Home Rule activism and Congress–League Lucknow Pact.
- 13 April 1919: Jallianwala Bagh massacre.
- 1920: AITUC founded; Non-Cooperation launched.
- February 1922: suspension after Chauri Chaura.
- December 1929: Purna Swaraj resolution at Lahore.
- March–April 1930: Dandi march and salt satyagraha.
- 1934: Congress Socialist Party established.
- 1935: Government of India Act.
- 1936: All India Kisan Sabha established.
- 8 August 1942: Quit India resolution endorsed in Bombay.
- 1946: Cabinet Mission and naval ratings' revolt.
- 1947: independence, Partition and mass displacement.

## Comparison cues

- **Non-Cooperation** withdraws collaboration; **Civil Disobedience** publicly breaches selected unjust laws.
- **Political independence** differs from **agrarian reform, labor rights and annihilation of caste**.
- **National unity** did not remove debate over minority representation and provincial autonomy.
- **Hyderabad State** was a princely state, not a British province; Telangana peasant struggle extends beyond 1947.

## Exam traps

- Do not say Quit India immediately expelled Britain; it was suppressed but was politically consequential.
- Do not equate Muslim religious identity with support for the Muslim League.
- Do not describe Partition violence as confined to a single region or social group.
- Distinguish original practice questions from authenticated TGPSC PYQs.

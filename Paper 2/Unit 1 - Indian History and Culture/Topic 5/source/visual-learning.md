# Visual Learning — Text diagrams for offline use

## Phase map

1885 Congress foundation → economic critique + constitutional agitation
        ↓
1905 Swadeshi → boycott + national education + assertive politics
        ↓
1916 Home Rule + Lucknow Pact → constitutional bargaining
        ↓
1919–22 Rowlatt / Non-Cooperation → mass action + debates over nonviolence
        ↓
1930–34 Salt / Civil Disobedience → lawbreaking + negotiation + social differences
        ↓
1935–37 Provincial autonomy → elections + experience of office
        ↓
1942 Quit India → arrests + decentralized resistance
        ↓
1946–47 Constitutional breakdown → transfer of power + Partition

## Causal analysis of Partition

British wartime weakness + provincial interests + separate representation
               + Congress–League disagreement + communal violence
                                 ↓
                   failed constitutional settlement
                                 ↓
                  compressed partition and transfer
                                 ↓
                    displacement and mass violence

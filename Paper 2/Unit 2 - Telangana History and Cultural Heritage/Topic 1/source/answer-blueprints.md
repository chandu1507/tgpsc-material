# Mains answer-writing blueprints — original teaching questions

## Q1. Analyze the contribution of Satavahanas, Ikshvakus and Vishnukundins to the cultural history of ancient Telangana.

Introduction: define ancient Telangana as geographic rather than fixed political region. Body: Satavahana trade, Prakrit and donations; Ikshvaku regional religious endowments; Vishnukundin grant politics and Brahmanical institutional patronage. Telangana evidence: Phanigiri, with comparison sites correctly located. Conclusion: continuity and change.

## Q2. Explain the significance of archaeological and epigraphic evidence for reconstructing the religious history of ancient Telangana.

Intro: inscriptions, artifacts and site plans provide complementary evidence. Body: Phanigiri; donors and inscriptional bias; site phasing; distinction of stupa/chaitya/vihara; other Jain evidence; geographic context. Conclusion: triangulation and uncertainties.

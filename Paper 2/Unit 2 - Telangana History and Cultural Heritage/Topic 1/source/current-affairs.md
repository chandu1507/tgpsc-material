# Current affairs: authenticated heritage research and published records

## Telangana Department of Heritage: Phanigiri excavation register (official public departmental record, consulted 22 October 2026)

The Department of Heritage, Government of Telangana, reports that at **Phanigiri in Suryapet district**, excavations in 2018–2019 exposed a 24-pillared congregation hall, a circular chaityagriha, an octagonal chaitya, several vihara units and a votive stupa. Its excavation summary also reports a **March 2024** discovery of a hoard of **3,730 lead coins** associated in the departmental account with the Ikshvaku period. These are reported excavation observations and interpretations—not independently re-dated artifacts by this author. The same government page references 2023 and 2024 international exhibitions of objects from Phanigiri.

**Source:** https://tgarch.telangana.gov.in/Excavationinfo (Government of Telangana, Department of Heritage; accessed 22 October 2026).

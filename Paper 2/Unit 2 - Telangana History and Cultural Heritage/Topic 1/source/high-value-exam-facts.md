# High-value syllabus revision and named evidence

## Dynasty–evidence–monument quick-reference
**Satavahanas:** epigraphic record including the **Nasik inscription of Gautami Balashri** concerning Gautamiputra Satakarni; coins, Prakrit and donor networks; associate broader lower Krishna art with the Deccan but do not relocate Amaravati to Telangana. **Ikshvakus:** patronage centered at **Vijayapuri/Nagarjunakonda**, in *present-day Andhra Pradesh*; women's endowments and Buddhist institutions are prominent. **Vishnukundins:** copperplate grants, royal Brahmanical patronage and early-medieval land rights; date-specific architectural attribution needs independent verification. **Telangana archaeological sites:** **Phanigiri**, **Dharmapuri** and other locally evidenced landscapes require chronology-conscious reading. Explain **stupa** (relic commemorative monument), **chaitya** (assembly/worship hall) and **vihara** (monastic residence) accurately. Do not conflate religious spread with population-wide conversion or assume all rulers were exclusive patrons of only one religion.

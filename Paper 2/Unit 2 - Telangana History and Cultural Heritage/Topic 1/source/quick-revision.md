# Quick revision: Ancient Telangana

| Theme | Exam-ready distinction |
|---|---|
| Geographic method | Present-day Telangana differs from ancient political jurisdictions. |
| Satavahanas | Prakrit inscriptions, varied Deccan reach, dynastic chronology debated. |
| Ikshvakus | Nagarjunakonda lies in present-day Andhra Pradesh; remains relevant as regional context. |
| Vishnukundins | Early medieval land grants and political realignment; copperplates are selective evidence. |
| Phanigiri | Important excavated Buddhist site within Suryapet, Telangana. |
| Buddhist architecture | Stupa (devotional monument), chaitya (worship space), vihara (monastic residence). |
| Jain history | Do not misdate later Jain heritage to earlier dynasties. |
| Historical method | A coin’s findspot is not automatic proof of direct rule. |

**Short causal chain:** agrarian and craft networks → wider exchange and political formations → donations and monastic institutions → regional artistic and literary practices → early medieval land-grant politics and changing patronage.

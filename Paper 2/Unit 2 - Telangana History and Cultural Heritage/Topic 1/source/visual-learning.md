# Visual comparison and conceptual diagrams

**Evidence chain:** Excavation context → dating analysis → monument / artifact interpretation → historical inference → cross-check with inscription or literary record.

**Schematic, not precise timeline:** Early Iron Age societies → early historic Satavahana spheres → successor courts and Ikshvaku networks → Vishnukundin-era political reorganization. Periods overlap and differ geographically.

| Source | What it establishes | What it cannot establish alone |
|---|---|---|
| Coin | Issuer, material, circulation context | Fixed kingdom boundary |
| Inscription | Claimed title, grant, donor | Impartial census |
| Stupa excavation | Sacred use and material phase | Universal religious identity |
| Copperplate | Stated grant terms | Land tenure in all districts |

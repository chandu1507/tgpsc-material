# Original mains blueprints — not official PYQs

## Examine the contribution of the Kakatiyas to social and cultural development in Telangana.

Introduce the dynasty and evidence limitations. Explain irrigation, urban Warangal, monuments, Telugu literary cultures and Nrtta Ratnavali; include Rudrama Devi and social hierarchy; close with community and artisan agency.

## How did the Qutb Shahis contribute to a composite culture in the Deccan?

Define composite culture using actual exchanges. Explain Ibrahim’s Telugu patronage; Muhammad Quli’s Dakhni poetry and Hyderabad founding; architecture and commercial networks; qualify with power and conflict.

## Discuss the significance of Sammakka–Sarakka and Sarvai Papanna resistance traditions.

Separate 13th-century Kakatiya-linked oral narrative from 17th–18th-century Papanna. Compare local autonomy, social hierarchy and historiographical evidence. No invented dates.

## Analyze the relationship between irrigation, political authority and settlement expansion under Kakatiya rule.

Relate ecology and reservoirs to agrarian surplus, maintenance, labor and distributional inequality. Integrate Pakhal and Ramappa as examples with attribution cautions; end with limits of architectural determinism.

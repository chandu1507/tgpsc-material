# Six original practice MCQs, not verified TGPSC PYQs

## Question 1: Which event is associated with the end of Kakatiya dynastic sovereignty at Warangal?

A. The Mughal conquest of Golconda in 1687
B. The 1323 capture of Warangal
C. The founding of Hyderabad in 1591
D. The 2021 Ramappa inscription

**Answer: B.** The fall of Warangal in 1323 ended the Kakatiya court’s sovereign rule; other events concern different periods.

## Question 2: Who is associated with the Sanskrit treatise Nrtta Ratnavali?

A. Jayapa Senani
B. Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah
C. Sarvai Papanna
D. Srinatha

**Answer: A.** Jayapa Senani is associated with the Kakatiya-era dance treatise.

## Question 3: Which monument received UNESCO World Heritage inscription in 2021?

A. Charminar
B. Qutb Shahi Tombs
C. Rudreshwara (Ramappa) Temple
D. Warangal Fort

**Answer: C.** UNESCO inscribed Ramappa Temple in 2021.

## Question 4: Which pairing is chronologically sound?

A. Sarvai Papanna—13th century
B. Muhammad Quli—Hyderabad foundation in 1591
C. Rudrama Devi—17th century
D. Ganapati Deva—18th century

**Answer: B.** Muhammad Quli founded Hyderabad in 1591; the other pairings conflate eras.

## Question 5: The strongest historical treatment of Sammakka–Sarakka recognizes:

A. Every oral detail as inscriptionally verified
B. A living oral and ritual tradition with uncertain exact medieval chronology
C. No relevance of oral evidence to history
D. The uprising as contemporary with Sarvai Papanna

**Answer: B.** Oral histories document memory and identity while the exact chronology needs independent corroboration.

## Question 6: Which statement about composite culture is most defensible?

A. It implies absence of all conflicts
B. It denotes traceable cultural exchange amid continuing differences
C. It began only with colonial rule
D. It excludes language and literary patronage

**Answer: B.** Exchange among arts and languages can coexist with inequality and contestation.

# Diagrams and comparisons

## Political and cultural chronology

Kakatiya expansion (12th–13th centuries) → capture of Warangal (1323) → successor Telugu chiefs / Velama courts → Bahmani–Deccan Sultanate context → Qutb Shahi Golconda (16th–17th centuries) → Mughal conquest (1687).

## Tanks and society

Rainwater storage → cultivation → agricultural surplus → settlements, military capacity and patronage; maintenance labor and unequal distribution influence actual outcomes.

## Source comparison

| Evidence | Strong for | Weak for |
|---|---|---|
| Inscription | Titles, donations, some dated events | Complete account of ordinary life |
| Standing monument | Material, workmanship, spatial form | Single-phase dating without excavation |
| Literary text | Patronage, vocabulary and courtly values | Literal proof of all narrated actions |
| Oral tradition | Community memory, values, lived meanings | Exact medieval chronology without corroboration |

# Answer-writing Blueprints - original mains practice

## Evaluate the nature of British paramountcy over Hyderabad State. How did it affect the Nizam's sovereignty?

1. Define paramountcy and distinguish it from direct rule.
2. Explain subsidiary alliances, Resident and military dependence.
3. Use the Hyderabad Contingent/Berar issue as fiscal-territorial evidence.
4. Show continued internal administration.
5. Conclude: layered sovereignty with shrinking strategic autonomy.

## Assess Salar Jung I's reforms as a process of modernization. Did they fundamentally transform Telangana society?

1. Start with fiscal-administrative crisis in 1853.
2. Explain district/revenue, judiciary, police, public works, education and communication changes.
3. Identify bureaucratic centralization.
4. Counterpoint: jagirdari/deshmukh power, vetti and rural inequality.
5. Conclude: state-capacity modernization, incomplete social transformation.

## Discuss the socio-economic development of Hyderabad State under the sixth and seventh Nizams, with special reference to Telangana.

1. Separate sixth and seventh reigns.
2. Cover railways/communications, water, industries and education.
3. Name Wadi-Secunderabad, Singareni, Osmania University and urban public works.
4. Balance city modernization with rural inequality.
5. Conclude with uneven development.

## Explain the historical significance of the 1857 Residency attack in Hyderabad and the role of Turre Baz Khan.

1. Set Hyderabad within all-India 1857 context.
2. Explain Nizam/Salar Jung support for British.
3. Describe 17 July attack by Turre Baz Khan/Maulvi Alauddin.
4. Assess scale carefully: significant resistance, not statewide takeover.
5. Conclude with fragmented princely-state responses to 1857.

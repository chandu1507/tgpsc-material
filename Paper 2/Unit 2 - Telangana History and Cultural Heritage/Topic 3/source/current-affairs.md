## September 2026: Telangana government seeks heritage-conservation cooperation

On **15 September 2026**, the Telangana State Portal reported that the Chief Minister met Prince Rahim Aga Khan V and discussed heritage conservation. The official release specifically referred to cooperation concerning the **historic City College and Osmania General Hospital buildings**, alongside other heritage initiatives. This is directly relevant to the Asaf Jahi chapter because both buildings belong to Hyderabad's late princely-era civic landscape.

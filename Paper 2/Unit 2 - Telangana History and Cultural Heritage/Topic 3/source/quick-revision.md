# Quick Revision - Asaf Jahi Telangana

## Core chronology

| Date / period | Event | Examination significance |
|---|---|---|
| 1724 | Asaf Jah I defeats Mubariz Khan at Shakar Kheda | Conventional beginning of autonomous Asaf Jahi rule |
| 1760s | Nizam Ali Khan consolidates and centers rule at Hyderabad | Political stabilization after succession struggles |
| 1798 / 1800 | Subsidiary alliance deepens | British military-diplomatic paramountcy |
| 1853 | Salar Jung I becomes Diwan; Berar assignment occurs | Reform era + fiscal cost of British military relationship |
| 17 July 1857 | Attack on British Residency led by Turre Baz Khan and Maulvi Alauddin | Hyderabad dimension of the 1857 resistance |
| 1869-1911 | Sixth Nizam Mahbub Ali Khan | Rail, institutions, bureaucracy expand |
| 1874 | Wadi-Secunderabad railway line | Market and imperial connectivity |
| 1911-1948 | Seventh Nizam Osman Ali Khan | Major public institutions, industry and urban modernization |
| 1918 | Osmania University established | Urdu-medium modern higher education and translation project |

## Five high-value distinctions

1. **Princely state != directly ruled British province.** Hyderabad retained internal rule but operated under British paramountcy.
2. **Modernization != democratization.** Bureaucracy and infrastructure expanded while political and agrarian inequalities persisted.
3. **Jagir != all landed power.** Legal revenue assignments, private landholding and local dominance must be distinguished.
4. **Vetti != ordinary taxation.** It denotes coercive/unpaid labour relationships with strong caste-class dimensions.
5. **Hyderabad city != all Telangana.** Urban public works should not be generalized to rural living conditions.

## 200-word answer framework

Opening definition -> political chronology -> one institutional reform -> one social/economic contradiction -> Telangana-specific example -> balanced judgment.

# Visual learning - Asaf Jahi Telangana

## 1. Political dependency chain

Mughal Deccan office -> Asaf Jah I's autonomous control (1724) -> succession struggles -> British military alliances -> subsidiary alliance -> Residency influence -> fiscal leverage / Berar -> princely internal rule under paramountcy

## 2. Social structure lens

Nizam / central departments
    |
Nobles / jagirdars / major officials
    |
Regional intermediaries such as deshmukhs and revenue officers
    |
Village officials, creditors and substantial cultivators
    |
Tenant cultivators / agricultural labourers / service communities

**Caution:** This is a conceptual hierarchy, not one legal structure valid in every district.

## 3. Modernization versus social change

| Modernizing institution | New capacity | Persistent contradiction |
|---|---|---|
| Central departments | Standardized administration | Autocratic political structure |
| Railways | Faster trade and mobility | Uneven regional benefit |
| University / colleges | Professional education | Unequal access and language politics |
| Industry | Wage employment / state revenue | Agrarian economy remained dominant |
| Urban water works | Flood control / municipal supply | Did not solve rural irrigation inequality |

## 4. 1857 Hyderabad causal map

British paramountcy + princely strategic dependence -> Nizam/Salar Jung support British

At the same time:

Anti-British sentiment + armed mobilization -> Turre Baz Khan / Maulvi Alauddin -> attack on Residency (17 July 1857) -> suppression -> enduring resistance memory

# Current Affairs and Contemporary Relevance

## Bhagyareddy Varma in official public memory
Telangana Prisons & Correctional Services reported observing Bhagyareddy Varma’s birth anniversary on **22 May 2025**, linking his legacy to justice, equality and human dignity. Source: https://prisons.telangana.gov.in/Acts/Prison%20CHRONICLE%20May%202025.pdf

## Telangana SEEEPC Survey report, 2026
A 2026 Telangana government socio-economic survey report discusses caste-feudal practices including vetti and notes Bhagyareddy Varma’s role in anti-caste organization through the Jagan Mitra Mandali and Adi-Hindu Social Service League. Source: https://des.telangana.gov.in/publications/SEEEPC/SEEEPC-IEWG%20Volume-1%2017-04-26.pdf

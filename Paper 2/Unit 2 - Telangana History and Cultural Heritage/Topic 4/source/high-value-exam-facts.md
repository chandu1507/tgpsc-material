# High-value syllabus revision and named evidence

## Named Telangana social-awakening institutions
**Andhra Jana Sangham** (formed in 1921 in Hyderabad context), later **Andhra Mahasabha**, served linguistic, library and political mobilisation in Telangana. The **Sri Krishnadevaraya Andhra Bhasha Nilayam** (1901, Hyderabad) is a specific library movement institution—not an interchangeable label for every society. **Andhra Saraswata Parishat** became important for Telugu education/literary culture; final year and foundational records need cross-checking. **Bhagya Reddy Varma** advanced Adi-Hindu mobilisation and education; **Madapati Hanumantha Rao**, **Suravaram Pratapareddy**, **Burgula Ramakrishna Rao** and women's associations warrant individual treatment by activity and date. A strong answer distinguishes library access, language rights, anti-caste reform, women’s education, political participation and the changing relationship of these associations to the Nizam's rule.

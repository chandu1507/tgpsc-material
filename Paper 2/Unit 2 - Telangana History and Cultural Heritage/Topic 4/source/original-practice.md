# Original Practice — NOT official PYQs

## Q1. Explain how literary and library movements contributed to political awakening in Telangana.
Intro: cultural public sphere under restricted politics. Body: reading rooms, language, lectures, networks, Andhra Jana Sangham, Andhra Mahasabha; transition to rural/social questions. Counterpoint: unequal access. Conclusion: organizational infrastructure for politics.

## Q2. Assess Bhagyareddy Varma’s role in the Adi-Hindu and Dalit movements of Hyderabad State.
Intro: anti-caste self-representation. Body: dignity, education, organization, public communication, institutional legacy. Distinguish self-assertion from paternalistic reform. Conclusion: early autonomous Dalit public politics.

## Q3. Why was the Vande Mataram movement important in Hyderabad State?
Intro: student protest and civil liberties. Body: educational restrictions, symbolism, nationalist link, repression, politicization. Caveat exact figures. Conclusion: bridge between campus and constitutional politics.

## Q4. Trace the transformation from socio-cultural awakening to demands for responsible government in Hyderabad.
Body sequence: print/libraries → linguistic association → social reform → Andhra Mahasabha → student/women activism → Hyderabad State Congress. Explain non-linear transition and state response.

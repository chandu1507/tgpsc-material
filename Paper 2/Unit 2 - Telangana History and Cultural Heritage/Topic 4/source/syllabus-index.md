# Official Syllabus Mapping

Socio-Cultural Awakening in Telangana - Andhra Sarasvath Parishat; Literary and Library Movements; Establishment of Nizam Rashtra Andhra Jana Sangham; Andhra Mahasabha; Social Reform Movements - Brahma Samaj, Arya Samaj and Adi-Hindu and Dalit Movements; Role of Bhagyareddy Varma; Andhra Mahila Sabha and the growth of Women’s Movement; Role of Hyderabad State Congress and Vande Mataram Movement.

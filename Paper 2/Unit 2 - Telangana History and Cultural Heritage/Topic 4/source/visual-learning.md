# Visual Learning

## Transformation map

Education + print → libraries and literary associations → social reform / anti-caste assertion → women and students in public life → civil-rights politics → responsible-government movement

## Distinguish organizations

| Type | Examples | Primary historical function |
|---|---|---|
| Literary / language | Andhra Jana Sangham, Andhra Saraswatha Parishat | language, literature, education |
| Broad socio-cultural forum | Andhra Mahasabha | cultural platform that widened into political/rural questions |
| Reform / self-respect | Arya Samaj, Adi-Hindu movement | religious/social reform, caste dignity |
| Women | Andhra Mahila Sabha and related associations | education, welfare, public participation |
| Student | Vande Mataram agitation | expression, nationalism, civil liberties |
| Political | Hyderabad State Congress | responsible government and civil rights |

## Telangana Praja Palana Dinotsavam — 17 September 2026
The Telangana State Portal reported the official 2026 Praja Palana Dinotsavam celebrations at Public Gardens, Hyderabad. The Chief Minister’s address described **17 September 1948** as the date Hyderabad State officially became an integral part of India and framed the transition as one from princely rule toward democratic governance. It also invoked Telangana’s historical resistance to oppression and landlord dominance.

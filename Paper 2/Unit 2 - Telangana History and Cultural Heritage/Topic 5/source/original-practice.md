## Q1. Explain the agrarian causes and consequences of the Telangana peasant armed struggle.
Structure: tenancy/land concentration/vetti → Andhra Mahasabha and communist organization → village sanghams and armed phase → repression → impact on agrarian politics and land reform.

## Q2. Assess Komaram Bheem as an Adivasi resistance leader.
Structure: Gond social ecology → forest/revenue pressures → Jodeghat struggle → source caution → later symbol of Jal-Jangal-Zameen/resource rights.

## Q3. Why did the Hyderabad crisis culminate in Operation Polo in September 1948?
Structure: lapse of paramountcy → Nizam’s independence strategy → Standstill Agreement → internal democratic/agrarian movements → Razakar violence and security crisis → failed negotiations → military intervention.

## Q4. “17 September 1948 ended princely sovereignty but not Telangana’s social conflicts.” Discuss.
Separate integration from continuation of armed peasant struggle to 1951 and persistent agrarian inequalities.

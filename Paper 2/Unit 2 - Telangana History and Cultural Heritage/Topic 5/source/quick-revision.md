# Quick Revision — People’s Movement against Nizam Rule

## Chronology
- Nineteenth century: Ramji Gond resistance in northern Telangana/Adilabad region.
- 1940: Komaram Bheem killed at Jodeghat.
- 1946: Doddi Komaraiah’s death becomes a turning point in peasant mobilization.
- 1946–1951: Telangana peasant armed struggle, with changing phases.
- 1947: British paramountcy lapses; Hyderabad does not immediately accede to India.
- 1947–48: Razakar expansion and deepening political crisis.
- 13–17 September 1948: Operation Polo / Police Action.
- 17 September 1948: Nizam announces cessation of resistance; integration process accelerates.
- 1951: communist armed struggle withdrawn.

## Distinguish clearly
Adivasi resource rights ≠ peasant class struggle ≠ Razakar politics ≠ Indian military integration, though these strands overlap historically.

## Core analytical themes
Land and tenancy · vetti · forest rights · political representation · princely sovereignty · communal polarization · decolonization · public memory.

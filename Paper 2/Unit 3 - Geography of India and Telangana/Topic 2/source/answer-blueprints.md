# Original Mains Blueprints — NOT official PYQs

## The Green Revolution solved a national food constraint but generated new regional and ecological challenges. Examine.

Blueprint: pre-Green Revolution food insecurity; technology package; regional concentration; food security gains; groundwater, nutrient and crop-diversity issues; next-generation productivity with resource efficiency.

## Explain how localisation factors differ among iron and steel, cotton textiles, automobiles, IT and food processing.

Blueprint: define localisation; compare weight-losing/raw-material, labour/market, cluster and perishability factors; explain technology/logistics changes; conclude agglomeration remains important.

## Transport infrastructure is both a consequence and a driver of regional economic development. Discuss.

Blueprint: network accessibility; roads vs rail vs ports; market-size and corridor effects; lagging-region connectivity; externalities and land/ecology; current 2026 examples.

## Can India automatically convert its changing age structure into a demographic dividend? Explain the geographical and policy conditions.

Blueprint: define demographic transition/dividend; regional fertility/age differences; conditions—health, education, jobs, women, migration, cities; risks—unemployment, ageing; conclusion conditional dividend.

# Current Affairs Integration — Official 2026 Sources

## Agriculture
The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare released Third Advance Estimates on 27 May 2026 placing 2025–26 foodgrain production at about **376.56 million tonnes**, with rice, wheat and maize at record levels in the estimate. Use as evidence of aggregate production growth, but do not infer farmer-income or ecological outcomes from output alone.
Source: PIB, 27 May 2026, Third Advance Estimates. https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2265965

## Roads and railways
A 2026 Government of India infrastructure factsheet reported about **146,572 km of National Highways** by March 2026 and **99.6% rail electrification** by July 2026. Use these figures as dated connectivity examples rather than permanent statistics.
Source: PIB infrastructure factsheet, 2026. https://www.pib.gov.in/FactsheetDetails.aspx?Id=150842

## Ports
Major ports handled **915.17 million tonnes** of cargo in FY2025–26; a later maritime factsheet reported about **1,668 million tonnes** across major and non-major ports. Use to discuss port capacity, hinterland connectivity and changing trade volumes.
Sources: PIB, 5 April 2026 and 2026 maritime factsheet.

## Industrial corridors
A 2026 government backgrounder on National Industrial Corridor Development listed multiple corridor projects and nodes including Hyderabad-linked corridors. Corridor announcements, completed nodes and actual investment outcomes must be distinguished.

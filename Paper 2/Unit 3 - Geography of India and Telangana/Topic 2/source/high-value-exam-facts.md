# High-value syllabus revision and named evidence

## Agriculture and economic geography: unique case linkages
**Green Revolution:** Punjab–Haryana–western UP gains rested on HYV seed, fertiliser, irrigation, procurement and extension; associated groundwater stress must be distinguished from yield gains. **Agro-climatic zone:** not identical to an administrative state and is defined by soil, temperature, rainfall and growing-season length. **Crop geography:** rice–canal/aquifer relations, cotton–black-soil association with regional exceptions, sugarcane–water/processing distance, millets–rainfall resilience. **Industrial localisation:** Jamshedpur's iron/steel resource history, Ahmedabad–Mumbai textiles, coastal port manufacturing and Hyderabad pharma/IT, with distinct factor bundles. **Trade:** ports and WTO rules act through market access, logistics, tariff and standards channels; they do not dictate one direction of Indian trade. **Urbanisation:** census town reclassification versus migrant flows, transport–housing accessibility and megacity externalities; **Smart Cities Mission** should be treated as a programme framework, not proof that specified outcomes occurred.

# Quick Revision — India: Agriculture, Industry, Transport, Population and Urbanisation

1. Crop geography = agro-ecology + irrigation + markets + policy.
2. Green Revolution was a technological-institutional package, not seed alone; spatial gains and environmental costs were uneven.
3. Irrigation potential is not the same as effective farm delivery; command-area management matters.
4. Industry localisation weights raw material, market, labour, energy, logistics, agglomeration and policy differently by sector.
5. Industrial corridors are networked development systems, not factories lined along one road.
6. Roads provide flexibility/last-mile access; rail excels at high-volume long-distance movement; ports integrate hinterlands with global routes.
7. Indian Ocean location links India to critical energy and container sea lanes.
8. Demographic dividend is conditional on skills, health, jobs and female participation.
9. Urbanisation brings agglomeration economies and congestion/exclusion/environmental costs simultaneously.
10. Smart-city answers should start with governance and basic services, then digital technology.

## 2026 anchors
- 2025–26 Third Advance Estimates: foodgrain production about **376.56 million tonnes**.
- National Highways: about **146,572 km** by March 2026.
- Rail electrification: **99.6%** by July 2026 according to a government infrastructure factsheet.
- Major Ports: **915.17 MT** cargo in FY2025–26; all major + non-major ports about **1,668 MT**.

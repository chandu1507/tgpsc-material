# Visual Learning

## Farm-to-market system
Agro-climate → crop choice → water/inputs → harvest → storage/processing → logistics → market/price → next-season decision

## Industrial location
Raw material + energy/water + labour/skills + market + transport/ports + policy/land → location → cluster economies

## Transport-development feedback
Better connectivity → lower time/logistics cost → larger effective market → investment/agglomeration → higher network demand

## Demographic dividend
Working-age share + health + education + skills + jobs + women’s participation → potential productivity gain

## Urban sustainability
Land use ↔ transport ↔ housing ↔ water/drainage ↔ jobs ↔ municipal finance/governance

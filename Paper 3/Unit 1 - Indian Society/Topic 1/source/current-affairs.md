# Topic-linked current affairs — data and society

## Telangana SEEEPC household survey — launched November 2024

The Telangana Government's Centre for Good Governance documents the launch of its *Socio-Economic, Education, Employment, Political and Caste (SEEEPC) Survey* in **November 2024**, using household-level data collection with the state Planning Department as nodal agency. The relevant sociological issue is the ability to study the interaction of occupation, education, family structure, caste, location and political participation, rather than infer lived conditions from a single category.

**Why this is examinable:** (1) multidimensional social stratification; (2) survey method versus census; (3) inclusion, privacy and data quality; (4) policy targeting; (5) migration and household-count challenges.

**Source checked:** Centre for Good Governance, Telangana, SEEEPC Survey project description: https://www.cgg.gov.in/it_project/socio-economic-education-employment-political-and-caste-seeepc-survey/ . This verifies the survey's existence and broad methodology; it does not certify every statistic later reported about its findings.

## Social composition and the importance of data years

The Office of the Registrar General and Census Commissioner publishes the **2011 C-16 mother-tongue tables** and **C-17 bilingual/trilingual tables**. Their reference year is 2011; an answer must never present a figure based on those tables as measured in 2026. The Government of Telangana has also published a **Socio-Economic Outlook 2026**. Use its clearly dated definitions and its relevant social-development indicators in answers only after verifying the exact table and year.

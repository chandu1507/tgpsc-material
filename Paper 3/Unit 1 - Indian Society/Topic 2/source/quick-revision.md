# High-value quick revision — Social Exclusion and Vulnerable Groups

## 90-second recall ladder

1. **Definition:** structural barriers to dignity, participation, resources and political voice; exclusion ≠ poverty.
2. **Mechanisms:** economic, social/cultural, political, spatial and digital; intersect across identities.
3. **Sociological names:** Ambedkar — graded inequality; Weber — social closure; Sen — capabilities; Crenshaw — intersectionality.
4. **SC:** Article 341 identification; Article 17 abolishes untouchability; PCR Act 1955; SC/ST PoA Act 1989.
5. **ST:** Article 342; Article 244(1)/Fifth Schedule; PESA 1996; FRA 2006 — individual **and community** rights.
6. **BC/OBC:** Mandal Commission 1979; Indra Sawhney 1992; NCBC Article 338B; 102nd Amendment 2018; 105th Amendment 2021; Central and state lists are distinct.
7. **Minority:** Article 29 any qualifying section with distinct language/script/culture; Article 30 religious and linguistic minority educational institutional rights.
8. **Women:** unpaid care, autonomy and safety; Domestic Violence Act 2005; POSH 2013; Article 15(3).
9. **Children:** Article 21A ages 6–14; RTE 2009; POCSO 2012; JJ 2015; child marriage 2006.
10. **Aged:** Article 41 directive principle; Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act 2007.
11. **Disability:** RPwD Act 2016; employment **4%**, government/government-aided higher education **5%** for benchmark disabilities; reasonable accommodation and universal design.
12. **Telangana:** forest-region service design, Hyderabad multilingual/urban exclusion, SEEEPC April 2026 survey volumes; avoid unverified caste percentages.

## Confusion busters

| Confused terms | Accurate distinction |
|---|---|
| Poverty / exclusion | Low resources / systemic barriers to participation |
| Equality / equity | Abstract identical treatment / fair response to unequal starting conditions |
| SC / ST | Caste-status/untouchability history / distinct tribal identity, cultural and resource governance issues |
| OBC / EWS | Socially and educationally backward class category / economic disadvantage classification |
| FRA / PESA | Forest tenure and resource rights / Scheduled Area panchayat self-government design |
| Article 29 / Article 30 | Any qualifying distinct language/script/culture section / educational institutional rights for religious and linguistic minorities |
| Disability 4% / 5% | Government employment / specified higher-education seats |
| Law enacted / law effective | Normative/statutory framework / measurable implementation and outcomes |
| Reservation / inclusion | One instrument of representation / multi-institutional effective participation |
| Telangana SEEEPC / legal reservation rules | Survey evidence / operative law, notification and service rules |

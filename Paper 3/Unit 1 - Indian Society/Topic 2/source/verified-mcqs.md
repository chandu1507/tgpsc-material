# Topic-specific MCQs — original practice, NOT official PYQs

**Authentication notice:** No commission-issued question wording and official answer key were authenticated for this exact topic during this production pass. All questions below are newly prepared for conceptual practice.

1. **Which proposition best differentiates social exclusion from poverty?**
   - A. Both mean only low household income.
   - B. Exclusion concerns structural barriers to equal participation; poverty concerns inadequate resources.
   - C. Exclusion occurs only among unemployed persons.
   - D. Poverty always ends when discrimination laws are passed.
   **Answer: B.** One may be excluded despite a high income, and anti-discrimination rules do not automatically remove deprivation.

2. **Which Article specifically abolishes untouchability?**
   - A. 17
   - B. 21A
   - C. 30
   - D. 41
   **Answer: A.** Article 17 is the constitutional abolition provision; Article 21A concerns education.

3. **Which pair is correctly matched?**
   - A. FRA 2006 — only public job reservation.
   - B. PESA 1996 — Scheduled Area self-government issues.
   - C. Article 342 — OBC Central List.
   - D. Article 338B — National Commission for Women.
   **Answer: B.** FRA covers a range of forest rights, Article 342 concerns ST identification, and 338B covers NCBC.

4. **The constitutional status of NCBC followed which amendment?**
   - A. 73rd, 1992
   - B. 86th, 2002
   - C. 102nd, 2018
   - D. 105th, 2021 exclusively
   **Answer: C.** The 102nd Amendment introduced Article 338B; 105th later clarified state list-making powers.

5. **Article 29(1) primarily concerns:**
   - A. The right to constitutional remedies exclusively.
   - B. Cultural conservation for a qualifying section with distinct language, script or culture.
   - C. Appointment of the NCBC.
   - D. Only religious minority educational institutions.
   **Answer: B.** Article 30 is the distinct religious/linguistic minority educational-institution provision.

6. **Under the RPwD Act, 2016, which statement is accurate in the stated statutory contexts?**
   - A. 5% government employment and 4% government-aided higher education.
   - B. Both contexts mandate 10%.
   - C. Not less than 4% specified government employment vacancies and not less than 5% specified higher-education seats.
   - D. No reservation provisions exist.
   **Answer: C.** Section 34 and Section 32 concern different institutional reservations, with eligibility conditions.

7. **Article 21A's constitutional education age band is:**
   - A. 0–5
   - B. 6–14
   - C. 14–21
   - D. All ages by direct constitutional wording.
   **Answer: B.** The right to free and compulsory education is constitutionally framed for ages six to fourteen.

8. **Which is a community-rights dimension that a discussion of FRA 2006 should not omit?**
   - A. Community forest resource management and use rights.
   - B. A universal 50% jobs reservation.
   - C. Automatic transfer of all national parks.
   - D. A single uniform tribal language requirement.
   **Answer: A.** The Act recognises multiple individual and community forest rights for eligible groups.

9. **Which indicator is least sufficient on its own to prove disability inclusion?**
   - A. Verified end-to-end accessibility audit.
   - B. Successful independent service usage.
   - C. Mere count of installed ramps.
   - D. Accessible grievance resolution.
   **Answer: C.** A ramp count may conceal obstacles to actual use.

10. **The most appropriate treatment of April 2026 SEEEPC survey volumes in mains writing is:**
    - A. Reproduce forwarded percentages without checking definitions.
    - B. Use them as official published material, checking methodology, version and universe before numerical claims.
    - C. Treat survey tables as new reservation laws.
    - D. Assume all district subgroups have identical outcomes.
    **Answer: B.** Publication establishes a primary source, but valid interpretation requires denominators, definitions and an operative legal distinction.

## Additional original four-option practice MCQs (11–25; 30 October 2026)

11. **A district officer applies PESA's Gram Sabha provisions. Which jurisdictional check is essential first?**
   - A. Whether the village has any ST residents, irrespective of legal status.
   - B. Whether the district is urban or rural, without checking notified areas.
   - C. Whether every village in the state is automatically covered.
   - D. Whether the village falls within a constitutionally notified Scheduled Area to which PESA applies.
   **Answer: D.** D identifies the Scheduled-Area scope. A confuses population with territorial notification; B omits the controlling legal boundary; C falsely universalises PESA. [S02]

12. **Under the Forest Rights Act recognition process, which institutional sequence is most accurate?**
   - A. Forest Department alone grants all titles without village scrutiny.
   - B. A district collector initiates claims and the Gram Sabha only receives copies.
   - C. Gram Sabha initiates/verifies claims, with committee review and final District Level Committee decision.
   - D. Parliament individually approves every village's claim.
   **Answer: C.** C reflects the statutory multi-tier process. A excludes statutory local participation; B reverses initiation; D invents parliamentary case-by-case approval. [S01]

13. **Which FRA provision most directly concerns a community's right to protect, regenerate, conserve or manage its customary community forest resource?**
   - A. Article 21A of the Constitution.
   - B. RPwD Act section 34.
   - C. PESA section 5 alone.
   - D. FRA section 3(1)(i).
   **Answer: D.** D names the community forest resource clause. A concerns education; B concerns disability employment reservation; C is not the FRA community forest resource provision. [S01]

14. **Which activity is specifically recognised among community entitlements under FRA section 3(1)(c)?**
   - A. Ownership, access to collect, use and dispose of traditionally collected minor forest produce.
   - B. Automatic private ownership of every protected forest.
   - C. Unconditional right to mine any mineral without other law.
   - D. A universal right to sell reserved public-sector posts.
   **Answer: A.** A tracks the minor forest produce entitlement. B invents universal ownership; C ignores separate mineral and environmental laws; D concerns employment rather than forest tenure. [S01]

15. **Under PESA section 4(e), which body is entrusted with approving village-level plans, programmes and projects for social and economic development before implementation?**
   - A. Only the national Parliament.
   - B. The Gram Sabha.
   - C. Only the State Public Service Commission.
   - D. The State Election Commission.
   **Answer: B.** B follows PESA's Gram Sabha role. A confuses legislation with village approval; C deals with recruitment; D administers elections, not development-plan approval. [S02]

16. **RPwD Act section 31 extends free education for children with benchmark disabilities to which statutory age band?**
   - A. Only 0–6 years.
   - B. Only 6–14 years, without extension.
   - C. Only 14–21 years.
   - D. Six to eighteen years in the statutory setting.
   **Answer: D.** D is the section 31 band. B confuses it with Article 21A/RTE's 6–14 band; A and C misstate the RPwD provision. [S03]

17. **RPwD Act section 32 sets what minimum reservation in covered government and government-aided higher education institutions for persons with benchmark disabilities?**
   - A. Five per cent of seats.
   - B. Four per cent of seats, identical to the employment clause.
   - C. Ten per cent of seats.
   - D. No statutory minimum.
   **Answer: A.** A is the covered higher-education minimum. B swaps the section 34 employment figure; C is unsupported; D denies the provision. [S03]

18. **RPwD Act section 34 generally specifies what minimum share of vacancies in covered government establishments for persons with benchmark disabilities?**
   - A. Five per cent in all private firms.
   - B. Exactly half of every cadre.
   - C. Not less than four per cent in the specified government-employment framework.
   - D. No provision for government employment.
   **Answer: C.** C is the statutory employment figure subject to section-specific terms. A conflates education and private employment; B is invented; D is false. [S03]

19. **An employee alleges disability discrimination in a government establishment. Which RPwD provision addresses appointment of a Grievance Redressal Officer?**
   - A. Section 32 (higher-education seats).
   - B. Section 34 (reservation).
   - C. Section 31 (free education).
   - D. Section 23 (Grievance Redressal Officer).
   **Answer: D.** D identifies the grievance provision. A, B and C concern distinct entitlements and are not the officer-appointment section. [S03]

20. **For accessibility standards under the RPwD Act, which section is the most direct starting point?**
   - A. Section 17 alone, which sets every building code.
   - B. Section 40, concerning rules for accessibility standards.
   - C. Section 58, which only defines transport routes.
   - D. Section 92, which is the sole accessibility rule.
   **Answer: B.** B identifies the standards provision. A concerns inclusive-education measures; C concerns disability certification; D concerns punishment for specified offences. [S03]

21. **Under RTE Act section 13, which school-admission practice is prohibited?**
   - A. Collecting capitation fees or using screening procedures for admission.
   - B. Admitting children without entrance interviews.
   - C. Providing age-appropriate support.
   - D. Maintaining school records.
   **Answer: A.** A states the prohibited practices. B describes avoiding screening; C supports inclusion; D is not itself the section 13 prohibition. [S04]

22. **Under RTE Act section 14(2), how should a school respond when a child lacks documentary proof of age?**
   - A. Refuse admission until a birth certificate is issued.
   - B. Require the child to repeat preschool indefinitely.
   - C. Impose a special admission tax.
   - D. Do not deny admission solely because proof of age is unavailable.
   **Answer: D.** D states the explicit safeguard. A is directly prohibited; B and C invent barriers absent from the provision. [S04]

23. **For POCSO Act section 2(1)(d), 'child' means:**
   - A. Only persons below 14.
   - B. Only girls below 18.
   - C. Any person below eighteen years.
   - D. Only persons enrolled in school.
   **Answer: C.** C follows the definition. A narrows age; B wrongly limits sex; D wrongly links protection to schooling. [S05]

24. **Under the Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act, 2007, section 5, which statement about initiating maintenance proceedings is accurate?**
   - A. Only a police officer can file an application.
   - B. An eligible parent/senior citizen may apply; an authorised person or organisation may act if incapable, and the Tribunal may take suo motu cognizance.
   - C. A child must be under 18 to apply on behalf of every elder.
   - D. Proceedings can begin only after a civil-court decree.
   **Answer: B.** B follows section 5's alternatives. A invents police exclusivity; C is unrelated; D adds a precondition not in the provision. [S06]

25. **Under section 4 of the 2007 Senior Citizens Act, against whom may a childless eligible senior citizen seek maintenance under the statutory conditions?**
   - A. A qualifying relative as defined by the Act.
   - B. Any random neighbour, irrespective of relationship.
   - C. Only the local school principal.
   - D. Only the Election Commission.
   **Answer: A.** A reflects the statutory relative category and conditions. B, C and D are not statutory maintenance obligors merely by being nearby or public authorities. [S06]

# Topic-specific MCQs — original practice, NOT official PYQs

**Authentication notice:** No commission-issued question wording and official answer key were authenticated for this exact topic during this production pass. All questions below are newly prepared for conceptual practice.

1. **Which proposition best differentiates social exclusion from poverty?**
   - A. Both mean only low household income.
   - B. Exclusion concerns structural barriers to equal participation; poverty concerns inadequate resources.
   - C. Exclusion occurs only among unemployed persons.
   - D. Poverty always ends when discrimination laws are passed.
   **Answer: B.** One may be excluded despite a high income, and anti-discrimination rules do not automatically remove deprivation.

2. **Which Article specifically abolishes untouchability?**
   - A. 17
   - B. 21A
   - C. 30
   - D. 41
   **Answer: A.** Article 17 is the constitutional abolition provision; Article 21A concerns education.

3. **Which pair is correctly matched?**
   - A. FRA 2006 — only public job reservation.
   - B. PESA 1996 — Scheduled Area self-government issues.
   - C. Article 342 — OBC Central List.
   - D. Article 338B — National Commission for Women.
   **Answer: B.** FRA covers a range of forest rights, Article 342 concerns ST identification, and 338B covers NCBC.

4. **The constitutional status of NCBC followed which amendment?**
   - A. 73rd, 1992
   - B. 86th, 2002
   - C. 102nd, 2018
   - D. 105th, 2021 exclusively
   **Answer: C.** The 102nd Amendment introduced Article 338B; 105th later clarified state list-making powers.

5. **Article 29(1) primarily concerns:**
   - A. The right to constitutional remedies exclusively.
   - B. Cultural conservation for a qualifying section with distinct language, script or culture.
   - C. Appointment of the NCBC.
   - D. Only religious minority educational institutions.
   **Answer: B.** Article 30 is the distinct religious/linguistic minority educational-institution provision.

6. **Under the RPwD Act, 2016, which statement is accurate in the stated statutory contexts?**
   - A. 5% government employment and 4% government-aided higher education.
   - B. Both contexts mandate 10%.
   - C. Not less than 4% specified government employment vacancies and not less than 5% specified higher-education seats.
   - D. No reservation provisions exist.
   **Answer: C.** Section 34 and Section 32 concern different institutional reservations, with eligibility conditions.

7. **Article 21A's constitutional education age band is:**
   - A. 0–5
   - B. 6–14
   - C. 14–21
   - D. All ages by direct constitutional wording.
   **Answer: B.** The right to free and compulsory education is constitutionally framed for ages six to fourteen.

8. **Which is a community-rights dimension that a discussion of FRA 2006 should not omit?**
   - A. Community forest resource management and use rights.
   - B. A universal 50% jobs reservation.
   - C. Automatic transfer of all national parks.
   - D. A single uniform tribal language requirement.
   **Answer: A.** The Act recognises multiple individual and community forest rights for eligible groups.

9. **Which indicator is least sufficient on its own to prove disability inclusion?**
   - A. Verified end-to-end accessibility audit.
   - B. Successful independent service usage.
   - C. Mere count of installed ramps.
   - D. Accessible grievance resolution.
   **Answer: C.** A ramp count may conceal obstacles to actual use.

10. **The most appropriate treatment of April 2026 SEEEPC survey volumes in mains writing is:**
    - A. Reproduce forwarded percentages without checking definitions.
    - B. Use them as official published material, checking methodology, version and universe before numerical claims.
    - C. Treat survey tables as new reservation laws.
    - D. Assume all district subgroups have identical outcomes.
    **Answer: B.** Publication establishes a primary source, but valid interpretation requires denominators, definitions and an operative legal distinction.

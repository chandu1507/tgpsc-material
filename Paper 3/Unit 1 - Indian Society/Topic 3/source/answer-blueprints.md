# Mains answer blueprints: how to score through unique arguments

Each blueprint is tied to its specific question in the separate answer bank. Do not interchange these structures mechanically.

| Practice question | Introduction | High-value body components | Distinctive conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 Poverty and money | Differentiate monetary and capability deprivation | MPI dimensions and reference year; chronic/transient; Telangana rural and rental cases; measurement limitation | Judge outcomes through both income and service security |
| Q2 Falling UR | State labour-force denominator | PLFS usual vs CWS; LFPR/WPR; underemployment; job quality and Hyderabad skill mismatch | A job-quality dashboard is more useful than one rate |
| Q3 Child labour | Rights and intergenerational cycle | 2016 law's exceptions; adult income; home work; RTE and migrant school continuity | Sustained schooling + adult earnings, not rescue totals |
| Q4 Violence against women | Power and continuum of abuse | PWDVA/POSH distinction; economic dependency; reporting bias; survivor services | Measure usable protection and agency |
| Q5 Regionalism | Constitutional expression vs exclusion | 1956/1969/2014 Telangana sequence; water/jobs and dignity; new within-state gaps | Cooperative federalism with fair resident rights |
| Q6 Displacement | Physical vs economic loss | Cernea; non-titleholders; 2013 law's covered processes; livelihood audit | Capability restoration is the success metric |
| Q7 Agrarian distress | Multi-risk livelihood system | Rainfall, price, credit, wages, tenancy; circular migration; MGNREGA household unit | Farm viability + labour dignity + safe mobility |
| Q8 Hyderabad | Agglomeration with unequal costs | Historic urban layers; ORR; Metro first/last mile; Musi; GHMC vs HMDA | Ecological coordination + affordable access |

**Presentation technique:** Use a concrete thesis in the opening, 3–4 separate cause-and-effect paragraphs, one named institution or statute, one Telangana case and one testable implication. Avoid a long list of schemes disconnected from the question.

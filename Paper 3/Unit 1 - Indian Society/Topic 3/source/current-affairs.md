# Topic-linked current developments (dated and carefully qualified)

## 1. Telangana Socio-Economic Outlook 2026

The Telangana State Portal lists a **2026 Socio-Economic Outlook** in its annual reports catalogue. It can be used for updated sector, labour, social development, urbanisation and farming context. **Do not insert unverifiable district rates from memory**; the detailed report's measure and year must be checked before using a number. This is an official source, not proof that every desired indicator was measured in 2026.

## 2. Periodic Labour Force Survey: 2025 annual and 2026 monthly publications

MoSPI published the **Annual PLFS 2025 report in 2026** and monthly CWS tables for the 2026 reference period. The September 2026 MoSPI dashboard reports the all-India **CWS unemployment rate for persons aged 15+ as 5.0%**. This is **not** the Telangana rate, not a 2025 annual usual-status estimate, and not a youth-specific rate. These definitions matter in mains answers on educated unemployment and labour-market distress.

## 3. Poverty indicator and dated projection

NITI Aayog's **15 January 2024** discussion paper estimated India's multidimensional poverty headcount at **11.28% for 2022–23**. Its projected nature and the MPI's weighting are essential qualifying facts. There is no basis to present this as a direct enumeration of 2026 income poverty or as a complete explanation of inequality.

## 4. Child labour and rights-based governance

The Labour Ministry's official text of the **Child and Adolescent Labour Act**, reflecting the **2016 amendment**, is the controlling examination reference for general prohibitions and limited exemptions. It should be read with the RTE framework, rather than assuming educational enrollment automatically establishes a child's safety.

## 5. Development and displacement law

The official text of the **2013 land acquisition, rehabilitation and resettlement law** details objections, hearings and resettlement procedures for covered acquisitions. The exact regime and applicable state changes must be checked for a real project; the presence of an Act is not proof of successful rehabilitation.

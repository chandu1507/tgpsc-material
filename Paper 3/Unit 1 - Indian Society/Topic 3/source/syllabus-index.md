# Official syllabus and chapter map

**TGPSC Group-I, Notification 02/2024, Annexure II, Paper III, Unit I, Topic 3.**

> Social Issues: Poverty; Unemployment, Child Labour, Violence against Women; Regionalism; Communalism and Secularism; Corruption; Caste Conflicts, Problems of Agricultural Labour; Urbanization; Development and Displacement; Environmental Degradation; Sustainable Development; Population Explosion; Agrarian Distress; Migration.

| Official clause | What a serious answer must cover | Detailed notes |
|---|---|---|
| Poverty | Monetary vs multidimensional; chronic vs transient; deprivation; MPI measurement; Telangana contrasts | Section 2 |
| Unemployment | PLFS usual/CWS, LFPR/WPR/UR; youth, women, job quality, skill matching | Section 3 |
| Child labour | 2016 amendment; 6–14 education; economic drivers; migration, family-work exceptions | Section 4 |
| Violence against women | Physical/economic/psychological harms; PWDVA 2005; POSH 2013; institutions | Section 5 |
| Regionalism | Constitutional demands vs exclusion; 1956, 1969, 2014 Telangana milestones | Section 6 |
| Communalism and secularism | Identity politics; Articles 14–16, 25–30; rights and accountability | Section 7 |
| Corruption | Petty/grand/state capture; PCA 1988/2018; institutional design | Section 8 |
| Caste conflicts | Graded inequality; dominant caste; atrocity law; land and local power | Section 9 |
| Agricultural labour | Seasonal wages, landlessness, MGNREGA, gender, worker protection | Section 10 |
| Urbanisation | Urban share vs population growth; primacy, informal housing, Musi | Section 11 |
| Development and displacement | Physical/economic displacement; Cernea; 2013 land acquisition law | Section 12 |
| Environmental degradation | Water, air, ecology, exposures, legal responsibilities and justice | Section 13 |
| Sustainable development | Brundtland, SDGs, strong/weak sustainability, trade-offs | Section 14 |
| Population explosion | TFR, demographic transition, momentum, ageing, rights | Section 15 |
| Agrarian distress | Crop vs price risk, net margins, debt, tenant farmers, Telangana | Section 16 |
| Migration | Circular vs permanent, portability, housing, wage protections | Section 17 |

**Exam pattern:** The official Paper III pattern gives approximately 200 words per 10-mark question. The student chapter contains original ~200–250-word models rather than unverified official past questions.

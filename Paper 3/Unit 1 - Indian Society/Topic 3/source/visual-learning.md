# Visual learning and analytical diagrams

## Social-issue feedback loop

```text
Erratic income / exclusion / ecological shock
               |
               v
         household debt
               |
        +------+-------+
        |              |
        v              v
  child work       circular migration
        |              |
        v              v
school disruption   insecure urban housing
        |              |
        +------+-------+
               v
      lower capabilities
               |
               v
        persistent poverty
```

## Policy logic hierarchy

```text
Diagnosis → Causal mechanism → Legal entitlement → Service delivery
   → Accessible remedy → Outcome indicator → Independent audit
```

## Population transition versus labour outcome

```text
Fertility declines → Working-age share may rise
                   → Jobs and women's work needed
                   → Potential demographic dividend
                   → Otherwise prolonged precarity
```

## Displacement accountability chain

```text
Project alternatives → Impact identification → SIA/hearing where required
→ Titleholders + tenants + common-resource users recognised
→ Replacement livelihoods, services, housing → multi-year monitoring
```

## Telangana case-link grid

| Setting | Mechanism | Appropriate metric |
|---|---|---|
| Dryland farming area | Weather + price shocks, credit | Real net farm income and debt-service burden |
| Rural labour market | Seasonal wage demand | Workdays, real wages, tail-end entitlements |
| Migrant urban settlement | Insecure tenure and service discontinuity | School continuity, rent/income, water access |
| Hyderabad western employment corridor | Matching, mobility, care work | Commute time, worker retention, women's participation |
| Musi catchment | Impervious land + drainage + pollution | Exposure, flood incidence and water quality |
| Reservoir-affected village | Economic displacement | Restored livelihood at year 1, 3 and 5 |

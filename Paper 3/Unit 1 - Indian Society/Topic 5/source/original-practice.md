# Ten original practice MCQs — not official PYQs

### MCQ 1

**Question:** Mission Shakti consists of which two sub-schemes?

**Answer:** Sambal and Samarthya. **Explanation:** The programme deliberately distinguishes protection/safety from empowerment.

### MCQ 2

**Question:** Which law governs specific child protection and care systems central to Mission Vatsalya?

**Answer:** Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act, 2015. **Explanation:** Mission Vatsalya supports structures created under the JJ framework.

### MCQ 3

**Question:** Article 21A principally covers free compulsory education for which age group?

**Answer:** 6–14 years. **Explanation:** Article 21A is the specific constitutional guarantee operationalised by RTE.

### MCQ 4

**Question:** Which law is most directly associated with individual and community forest rights?

**Answer:** Forest Rights Act, 2006. **Explanation:** PESA and FRA address different but related aspects of governance and rights.

### MCQ 5

**Question:** The central rights framework for disability is?

**Answer:** RPwD Act, 2016. **Explanation:** Reasonable accommodation and accessibility differ from an isolated welfare payment.

### MCQ 6

**Question:** Which is the strongest proxy for learning outcomes?

**Answer:** Foundational reading and numeracy attainment. **Explanation:** School building count alone cannot measure learning.

### MCQ 7

**Question:** Which environmental statute is 1986?

**Answer:** Environment (Protection) Act. **Explanation:** It supplies an overarching framework distinct from Water Act 1974 and Air Act 1981.

### MCQ 8

**Question:** Mission Poshan 2.0 primarily addresses?

**Answer:** Nutrition and early development. **Explanation:** It should not be confused with Mission Vatsalya’s child protection role.

### MCQ 9

**Question:** Population momentum means?

**Answer:** Continued growth despite lower fertility due to age structure. **Explanation:** Replacement fertility need not immediately halt population growth.

### MCQ 10

**Question:** Which is a more defensible welfare outcome?

**Answer:** Reduction in wrongful exclusions and sustained capability improvement. **Explanation:** Disbursement volumes measure programme activity, not necessarily social impact.

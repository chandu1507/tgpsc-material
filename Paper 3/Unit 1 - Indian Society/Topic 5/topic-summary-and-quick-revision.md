# Topic summary — Social Policies and Programmes

- **Core distinction:** a constitutional right, a statute, a subordinate rule, a centrally sponsored scheme and a state programme are not interchangeable.
- **Women:** Mission Shakti = Sambal (safety/protection) + Samarthya (empowerment). Domestic Violence Act and POSH Act operate differently.
- **Children:** Poshan 2.0 addresses nutrition/early development; Mission Vatsalya supports care and protection under the juvenile-justice framework.
- **SCs:** Article 17 and anti-atrocity protections complement educational and economic instruments.
- **STs:** Fifth Schedule, PESA and FRA concern differentiated governance and land/forest rights; PM-JANMAN focuses on PVTGs.
- **Backward Classes:** state BC list and Union OBC list are not automatically identical.
- **Minorities:** Article 29 is broader than minority-only protection; Article 30 concerns religious/linguistic minority educational institutions.
- **Aged:** pensions, accessible healthcare, long-term care and protections under the 2007 Act.
- **Disability:** RPwD 2016, reasonable accommodation, education and employment access—not certificates alone.
- **Environment:** EPA 1986, Water Act 1974, source pollution control, EIA, catchment protection and environmental justice.
- **Population:** National Population Policy 2000, voluntary choice, demographic momentum and ageing.
- **Education:** RTE, NEP 2020, Samagra Shiksha, PM POSHAN; learning outcomes must accompany access.
- **Health:** NHP 2017, NHM and Ayushman Bharat; primary care differs from hospital insurance.
- **Poverty:** MGNREGA, NFSA, housing and livelihood instruments solve different deprivation dimensions.

## 12-point quick revision

1. Name the **legal basis** and **implementing department**.
2. Distinguish **eligibility** from actual coverage.
3. State whether an intervention is **statutory**, **centrally sponsored**, or **state-specific**.
4. Separate policy **announcement** from operational evidence.
5. Verify the **reference year** for any statistic or amount.
6. Explain **last-mile exclusion** (documentation, migration, digital access).
7. Explain **intersectionality** using a concrete beneficiary profile.
8. Prefer indicators of **outcome**, not money spent.
9. Cite a named **Telangana example** accurately.
10. Contrast a **cash benefit** with service quality and institutional rights.
11. Use short mains **subheadings** for readability.
12. End with an accountable **delivery improvement** and success metric.

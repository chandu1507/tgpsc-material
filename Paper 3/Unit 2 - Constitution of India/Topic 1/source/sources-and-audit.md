# TGPSC P3 U2 T1 — Sources And Audit

## 8. Authoritative source register and internal checks

The source register lists documents consulted for legal or historical propositions. A source entry is **not** a claim that every passage of this draft has undergone full scholarly fact-checking. The official notification governs syllabus wording; the Constitution and statute govern legal text; judgments govern case holdings; explanatory websites do not override primary law. Source links are supplied without page-number citations in student-facing prose.

- **[S1] Constituent Assembly Debates, 29 August 1947 (digitised primary proceedings):** https://www.constitutionofindia.net/debates/29-aug-1947/ — verifies the resolution appointing the Drafting Committee and its original members.
- **[S2] Legislative Department, Government of India, Constitution of India (official English text):** https://www.legislative.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/07/359f70a69695affb9d72f8393102bd2e.pdf — Articles 1, 3, 4, 12–35, 36–51, 51A, 246 and 368, subject to official subsequent amendments.
- **[S3] Constitution of India archive, stages of constitution-making:** https://www.constitutionofindia.net/stages-of-constitution-making/ — chronology and document provenance.
- **[S4] Constituent Assembly debates, appointment debate and committee limits:** https://www.constitutionofindia.net/blog/this-month-in-constitution-making-august-1947-constituent-assembly-appoints-drafting-committee/ — context for expert committee versus Assembly authority.
- **[S5] National Archives of India, Constitution collection:** https://nationalarchives.nic.in/en/constitution-india — primary constitutional record.
- **[S6] Legislative Department, official amendment collection:** https://www.legislative.gov.in/amendments-to-the-constitution — source for amendment enactments and dates.
- **[S7] India Code, Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014:** https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2123/1/A2014-6E.pdf — sections 3, 10–12 and the legal route to Telangana.
- **[S8] Supreme Court of India, basic-structure discussion in a reported judgment:** https://api.sci.gov.in/judgment/judis/28469.pdf — judicial account of Kesavananda and Article 368 limits.
- **[S9] Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala, 24 April 1973:** authoritative case citation (1973) 4 SCC 225; verify the original reported opinions for detailed ratio and separate opinions.
- **[S10] PRS Legislative Research, summary of women's reservation constitutional proposal:** https://prsindia.org/billtrack/prs-products/prs-bill-summary-4251 — describes the 2023 amendment's census/delimitation-linked mechanism; distinguish Bill stage from enacted 106th Amendment.
- **[S11] Akashvani News on AIR, 18 April 2026:** https://newsonair.gov.in/hi/131st-constitutional-amendment-bill-not-passed-lok-sabha-vote-count/ — verifies 131st Amendment Bill's failure on 17 April 2026.
- **[S12] PRS Legislative Research, April 2026 constitutional amendment analysis:** https://prsindia.org/billtrack/prs-products/issues-for-consideration-1776322954 — context of proposed 2026 delimitation changes.
- **[S13] TGPSC Group I Notification 02/2024 (Commission-issued document, mirrored PDF):** https://www.careerpower.in/blog/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/19195508/NOTIFICATION_NO_02-2024_Group-I_Services.pdf — exact Paper III Unit II Topic 1 wording; a mirror of the original notification, not a Commission-hosted URL.

**Internal accuracy gates performed:** syllabus clause transcribed from the 02/2024 notification; Article 3 versus Article 368 route explicitly distinguished; 2026 131st Bill labelled unsuccessful rather than enacted; original questions never called official PYQs; major case holdings separated from their critical interpretations; original-versus-current Constitution counts distinguished; no fabricated page-number citations. **Outstanding:** line-by-line expert academic audit, comprehensive validation of every secondary historical claim, current consolidated amendment-text check beyond the cited edition, and independent checking against authenticated TGPSC question papers.

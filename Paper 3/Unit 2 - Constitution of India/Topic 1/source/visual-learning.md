### Table A. Sources of constitutional power

| Mechanism | Who acts? | Required procedure | Telangana / exam application |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary Union legislation | Parliament | Ordinary legislative process, subject to Constitution | Routine Union laws; not automatically constitutional amendments |
| State reorganisation, Arts 3–4 | Parliament, with Presidential recommendation and referral for State views | Ordinary law after specified consultation | Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014 |
| Article 368 amendment | Both Houses separately | Majority of total membership AND two-thirds of present and voting | Changes to constitutional text |
| Article 368 federal-provision amendment | Both Houses + State legislatures | Special majority plus ratification by at least half of States | Specified federal structure matters |
| Judicial constitutional interpretation | Constitutional courts | Decision in a properly brought case | Can change legal meaning, not the printed text by itself |

### Table B. Constitutional values: rule, conflict and consequence

| Value | Constitutional expression | Typical policy tension | Analytical test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Liberty | Fundamental Rights; Preamble | Public order versus dissent | Is restriction lawful, justified and reviewable? |
| Equality | Arts 14–16 and protective provisions | Formal sameness versus historical disadvantage | Does classification or affirmative measure satisfy constitutional standards? |
| Justice | Preamble and Directive Principles | Redistribution versus property/economic interests | Are both policy goal and means constitutional? |
| Democracy | Adult franchise; parliamentary accountability | Majority mandate versus opposition/minority rights | Are competition, information and checks meaningful? |
| Federalism | Arts 1, 3, 246; Seventh Schedule | National coordination versus State autonomy | Which body has competence, and what safeguards apply? |
| Judicial independence | Constitutional court architecture | Accountability versus insulation from politics | Does reform preserve independent adjudication? |

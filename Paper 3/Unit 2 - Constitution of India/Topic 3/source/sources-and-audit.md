## Internally checked legal propositions

1. **Collective responsibility:** Articles 75(3) and 164(2) point to Lok Sabha and State Assembly respectively. No-confidence cannot be moved against the ministry in Rajya Sabha or the Telangana Council.
2. **Cabinet ceiling:** Article 164(1A) says not exceeding 15% of Assembly membership, subject to a minimum of twelve. Telangana 119 × 0.15 = 17.85, so at most 17.
3. **President's electoral college:** Article 54 excludes elected MLCs and nominated MPs; inclusion of elected MLAs of Delhi and Puducherry checked against constitutional amendments.
4. **Bills:** Article 110/199 Money Bill definition includes 'only' listed matters; Article 117 covers other financial bills; Article 108 joint sitting is not for Article 368 amendments or Money Bills.
5. **State ordinary-bill delay:** Article 197 first stage three months and second stage one month; no state joint sitting. Council-origin bills are not protected by an independent Council override.
6. **Privilege:** *Sita Soren* is a seven-judge unanimous 2024 judgment. Immunity for a genuine vote does not extend to bribery.
7. **Governor assent:** The five-judge November 2025 Presidential Reference is controlling for this draft's summary. No blanket judicial three-month limit and no deemed assent; limited scrutiny of prolonged, unexplained inaction remains.
8. **Legislative sittings:** PRS 2025 review's 11-day Telangana Council figure is historical descriptive evidence, not a mandatory minimum.
9. **Telangana seats:** Official Telangana department reports 119 Assembly and 40 Council seats; avoid third-party claims of 43 Council seats or confusing sanctioned strength with filled vacancies.
10. **Original questions:** 25 new MCQs and six mains practice questions; zero official PYQ labels; no invented TGPSC question numbers.

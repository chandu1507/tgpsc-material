## Source-quality controls and unresolved items

1. **Official syllabus:** Original TGPSC PDF was indexed by search but returned HTTP 403 on direct retrieval; exact wording was corroborated by a public reproduction. A direct-PDF recheck is still needed before academic approval.
2. **2026 legal update:** India Code identifies Act 36 of 2025; the Ministry's 11 May 2026 notice gives 1 July 2026 commencement/repeal. Do not treat historical MGNREGA dashboard labels as proof the 2005 statute remains current.
3. **State-specific operational rules:** Do not assume Telangana has issued a particular new state employment scheme, wage rate or complaint regulation without the relevant current government order.
4. **Telangana schemes:** Housing and health amounts reflect dated official district pages; eligibility, coverage and packages can change. Government-reported totals are not causal evaluation.
5. **Legal nuance:** Health under Article 21 is an interpreted constitutional right, not unlimited free treatment; Article 21A covers ages 6–14; NFSA entitlement denominators are different for PHH and AAY.
6. **Practice questions:** All MCQs and mains prompts are newly written original practice questions; no official past question is asserted or simulated as verified.
7. **Academic approval:** Structural checks and source cross-checking are not a full subject-expert legal/factual audit. This chapter remains **NOT APPROVED**.

### Q26. An eligible company's unspent CSR amount relates to a qualifying ongoing project. Which immediate year-end step is ordinarily required under Section 135(6)?
A. Transfer it to the Unspent CSR Account within 30 days after the financial year ends.  B. Pay it as dividends within 30 days.  C. Transfer it to a Schedule VII fund within six months as if it were non-ongoing.  D. Carry it forward indefinitely without disclosure.

**Answer: A.** Section 135(6) requires the separate account and 30-day transfer for ongoing-project amounts [S09]. B confuses CSR with shareholder distribution; C substitutes the non-ongoing route; D ignores the statutory transfer duty.

### Q27. For an unspent CSR amount **not** related to a qualifying ongoing project, which statement follows Section 135(5)?
A. It must remain in the company's general account for three years.  B. It must be transferred to an eligible Schedule VII fund within six months of financial-year end.  C. It is automatically waived if the Board gives reasons.  D. It must be transferred to the Unspent CSR Account within 30 days.

**Answer: B.** Section 135(5) specifies the six-month Schedule VII route for non-ongoing amounts [S09]. A invents a general-account carry-forward; C treats explanation as waiver; D substitutes the ongoing-project account.

### Q28. Under Section 135(9), what happens when a covered company's required CSR expenditure does not exceed ₹50 lakh?
A. The company is exempt from all CSR spending.  B. The company may ignore CSR reporting.  C. A separate CSR Committee is not mandatory; the Board performs its functions.  D. Only an NGO may approve the CSR budget.

**Answer: C.** The exception concerns committee constitution, not the substantive CSR duty [S09]. A and B wrongly erase compliance; D invents NGO approval power.

### Q29. Under the DEA's revamped VGF social-sector Sub-scheme 1, which statement is accurate?
A. Every PPP automatically receives 80% of capital cost.  B. Only private companies approve grants.  C. The scheme prohibits sponsoring-authority support.  D. Eligible projects may receive up to 30% central capital support plus up to 30% additional eligible sponsoring-authority support, subject to conditions.

**Answer: D.** The conditional 30%+30% social-sector Sub-scheme 1 ceilings are published by DEA [S11]. A universalises a different category; B confuses bidder with approving authority; C denies expressly permitted co-support.

### Q30. Which statement matches RBI's 1 April 2024 SHG–Bank Linkage Master Circular?
A. Mature SHGs may receive loans beyond four times savings at the bank's discretion.  B. Every SHG is prohibited from borrowing above its savings.  C. SHG loans are automatically grants.  D. Every mature SHG has an unconditional right to unlimited credit.

**Answer: A.** RBI describes ordinary 1:1–1:4 savings-linked ratios but allows mature SHGs to exceed 1:4 at the bank's discretion [S14]. B invents a lower cap; C confuses loans with grants; D removes credit assessment.

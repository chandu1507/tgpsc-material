### Internally checked assertions

- The **CSR ₹500 crore / ₹1,000 crore / ₹5 crore** tests are alternatives and apply to the preceding financial year; the ordinary spending rule is **2% of average net profits**, not turnover [S09].
- CSR Exchange portal registration is not an independent endorsement of the organisation's quality [S09].
- **Indira Mahila Shakti** dates to 8 March 2025; the **553 buses** statement is a June 2026 government report, not a verified impact evaluation [S05, S08].
- VGF and PPP pipeline listings are not the same as completed projects [S11–S12].
- SERP is the rural SHG ecosystem and MEPMA the urban mission; the categories are not interchangeable [S06–S07, S15].
- The SDG composite figure is an index score; interpreting 71 as 71% of citizens attaining all SDGs would be wrong [S03].
- The 73rd and 74th Amendments create a constitutional framework; actual devolution is contingent on state implementation [S16].

### Table 1. Development paradigms

| Approach | Principal question | Strength | Main blind spot | Appropriate Telangana test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Growth/modernisation | Is productivity and output rising? | Financing capacity, structural change | Distribution, externalities | Are industrial jobs accessible beyond skilled urban workers? |
| Capability/human development | Are real freedoms expanding? | Health, learning, dignity | Measurement and fiscal trade-offs | Do girls complete education safely? |
| Redistribution/social justice | Who gains assets, income and voice? | Exposes unequal power | Can ignore productive base | Are tribal and landless groups included? |
| Sustainability | Can gains persist ecologically? | Climate, groundwater, future generations | Long horizons complicate budgets | Are lake and water systems resilient? |

### Table 2. Actors and accountability

| Actor | Comparative advantage | Principal risk | Correct accountability device |
|---|---|---|---|
| State department | Legal authority, taxation, universal mandate | Political/bureaucratic failure | Legislative scrutiny, audit, RTI, appeals |
| Competitive private firm | Innovation, cost discovery | Profit-driven exclusion, externalities | Regulation, competition, contract metrics |
| NGO/charity | Trust, specialisation, flexibility | Donor capture, weak representation | Audits, safeguarding, beneficiary grievance |
| CBO/Gram Sabha | Local knowledge, legitimacy | Elite capture, tokenism | Inclusion rules, open records, response to objections |
| SHG federation | Savings discipline, bargaining | Debt stress, leadership concentration | Member elections, accounts, market-outcome checks |
| CSR sponsor | Finance and expertise | Branding over public value | Section 135 disclosures, independent impact assessment |

### Table 3. PPP and procurement instruments

| Instrument | Typical payment source | Core private risk | Public safeguard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conventional works contract | Budget milestones | Construction/defects | Inspection and defect liability |
| BOT toll concession | Users, subject to contract | Construction and often demand | Tariff oversight, quality, renegotiation rules |
| Availability-payment PPP | Government subject to uptime | Construction and availability | Performance deductions, fiscal disclosure |
| Management contract | Government/user tariff | Management performance | Independent KPI verification |
| VGF-assisted concession | Users plus eligible grant | As contract allocates | Competitive bidding and VGF appraisal |

### Table 4. CSR legal quick test

| Question | Correct rule or distinction |
|---|---|
| Who is covered? | Any **one** of net worth ≥ ₹500 crore, turnover ≥ ₹1,000 crore, net profit ≥ ₹5 crore in preceding FY |
| Required spend? | Generally at least 2% of average net profits of preceding three FYs, calculated as prescribed |
| Statute? | Companies Act, 2013, Section 135; Schedule VII; CSR Rules, 2014 as amended |
| Is CSR identical to tax-deductible business expenditure? | No |
| Does portal registration certify implementing-agency quality? | No; corporate due diligence remains essential |
| Are CSR projects proof of public impact? | No; spending and independently measured outcomes differ |

# Question-specific mains answer blueprints

**Original practice only.** Each prompt below is newly authored; no official TGPSC PYQ provenance is claimed.

1. **Money supply and monetary aggregates:** Define money and liquidity → present M0/M1/M3 identities → explain bank-deposit creation → contrast stock and transaction volume → evaluate limitations of money growth as inflation predictor → conclude on RBI statistical use.
2. **Structure of Indian banking:** Establish institutional diversity → classify PSBs/private/foreign/RRB/cooperative/SFB/payments banks and NBFCs → compare licences and regulators → identify Telangana rural and urban requirements → balance reach against governance risk → conclude on proportionate regulation.
3. **Banking reforms and NPAs:** Explain pre-1991 problems → Narasimham committees, prudential standards, competition, mergers, IBC → use dated GNPA stock evidence correctly → discuss write-off versus recovery, fiscal and consumer trade-offs → conclude on sustainable governance.
4. **RBI credit regulation:** Define objectives → classify policy rate, corridor, CRR/SLR/OMO and selective/prudential instruments → trace transmission → consider supply shocks and inclusion → conclude on coordination and accountability.
5. **Financial inclusion and Telangana:** Explain exclusion mechanisms → roles of banks, RRBs, cooperatives, SHGs, correspondents, UPI → discuss tenant farmers and MSMEs → assess digital and over-indebtedness risks → propose outcome metrics → conclude on safe access.
6. **NBFCs and financial stability:** Explain non-bank intermediation → differentiate NBFCs from banks, deposits and DICGC → discuss funding mismatch, microfinance, scale-based regulation → evaluate access-versus-risk dilemma → conclude on proportionate oversight.

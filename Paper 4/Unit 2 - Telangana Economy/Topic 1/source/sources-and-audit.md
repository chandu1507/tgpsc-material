### Q3 — four-option evidence review (key B)

- **A:** 1889 is the start year of Singareni coal mining, not Pocharam's foundation.
- **B (correct):** Kamareddy district government states the Nizam laid Pocharam's foundation in 1917; construction completed in 1922.
- **C:** 1956 concerns the reorganisation of Andhra Pradesh, not Pocharam's foundation.
- **D:** 2014 is Telangana statehood, almost a century later.

### Q4 — four-option evidence review (key A)

- **A (correct):** SCCL's own Singareni Day account explicitly says coal mining started in 1889.
- **B:** 1920 marks incorporation of SCCL, not the commencement of mining.
- **C:** 1945 marks Hyderabad State's majority-share acquisition, not the start of mining.
- **D:** 1956 marks transfer of controlling interest following state reorganisation, not mining commencement.

### Q5 — four-option evidence review (key C)

- **A:** 1889 was mining commencement, not SCCL's legal incorporation.
- **B:** 1917 was Pocharam's foundation year and is unrelated to SCCL incorporation.
- **C (correct):** The company's own history states incorporation on 23 December 1920 under the Hyderabad Companies Act.
- **D:** 1948 is not the incorporation date in the company history; the 1945 ownership change is distinct.

### Q6 — four-option evidence review (key C)

- **A:** 1920 was incorporation of SCCL, not majority acquisition by Hyderabad State.
- **B:** 1930 is not the majority-acquisition year given in SCCL's official history.
- **C (correct):** The company's history explicitly dates the Hyderabad State purchase of majority shares to 1945.
- **D:** 1956 relates to controlling interest devolving on Andhra Pradesh, not Hyderabad State's purchase.

### Q14 — four-option evidence review (key B)

- **A:** 1 November 1956 belongs to the earlier Andhra Pradesh reorganisation period, not Telangana statehood.
- **B (correct):** The Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act's official footnote identifies 2 June 2014 as the notified appointed day.
- **C:** 15 August 1947 is India's independence, not the formation date of Telangana State.
- **D:** 1 January 2015 is not the notified appointed day under the 2014 Act.

### Q23 — four-option evidence review (key A)

- **A (correct):** NITI's 2023 MPI Telangana snapshot lists NFHS-5 (2019–21) headcount at 5.88%.
- **B:** 13.18% is the earlier NFHS-4 (2015–16) Telangana headcount, not NFHS-5.
- **C:** 68.6% is a stated 2025–26 tertiary GSVA share, not an MPI headcount.
- **D:** 15.1% is a stated 2025–26 primary GSVA share, not the historical MPI headcount.

### Q24 — four-option evidence review (key D)

- **A:** 7.30 is the percentage-point difference, not a 7.30% relative decline; 55.39 is not the percentage-point change.
- **B:** The percentages and percentage-point units are reversed; 13.18−5.88=7.30 percentage points.
- **C:** 13.18% and 5.88% are the survey endpoints, not the changes between them.
- **D (correct):** 13.18−5.88=7.30 percentage points; (7.30/13.18)×100≈55.39% relative decline. This is a historical NFHS comparison.

### Q25 — four-option evidence review (key B)

- **A:** GSDP divided by population gives a per-capita output concept, not multidimensional poverty.
- **B (correct):** The Alkire–Foster MPI is H×A, with headcount H and average deprivation intensity A expressed as fractions.
- **C:** Income-tax revenue divided by GDP is a fiscal ratio, not an MPI.
- **D:** Exports minus imports is net exports, not a measure of household deprivation.

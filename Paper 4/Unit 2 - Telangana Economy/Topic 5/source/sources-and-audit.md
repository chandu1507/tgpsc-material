## Claims checked and qualifications

1. **Exact syllabus:** Commission-hosted Group I syllabus text directly located; Notification 02/2024 corroboration available, but the notification PDF itself has not been fully authenticated line-by-line.
2. **2025 MSME thresholds:** Central limits recorded with effective-year distinction; precise operative date should be rechecked against Gazette if challenged.
3. **Services shares:** 62.4% GSVA and 34.8% workforce refer to 2023–24 in separate NITI analyses. They must not be relabelled as 2025–26 or 2026–27.
4. **Industrial incentives:** No universal current T-IDEA reimbursement rates asserted; scheme conditions may vary by order and project category.
5. **TS-iPASS:** No claim that all permissions universally have a fifteen-day deadline; approval category matters, and safety laws continue to apply.
6. **ICT and life-sciences targets:** Explicitly identified as targets or announcements, not achieved jobs or exports.
7. **SEO 2026:** Official publication identified, but its full industrial sub-sector tables not exhaustively audited; unsupported latest percentages excluded.
8. **Official PYQs:** Zero claimed. No source paper/question-number provenance established.
9. **Academic approval:** Zero; subject expert, legal currency and independent statistical audit remain pending.

# Source Register, Current Affairs and Audit

## Dated current-affairs integration (verified as of 24 October 2026)

1. **27 January 2026 / 1 April 2026 — Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026.** The Union notified the rules on 27 January; commencement was 1 April. The new framework superseded the general 2016 rules subject to savings. Distinguish four-stream source segregation and responsibilities from separate special-waste regimes. Primary: Gazette text, https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/jan/doc2026129773501.pdf . Status: **operative rules**; no claim that every municipality has achieved compliance.
2. **24 April 2026 — India's NDC for 2031–2035 submitted to UNFCCC.** Targets include 47% GDP emissions-intensity reduction relative to 2005, approximately 60% cumulative installed electric-power capacity from non-fossil sources, and a 3.5–4.0 billion-tonne CO2e sink through forest/tree cover by 2035. Primary: UNFCCC registry, https://unfccc.int/node/656817 and the submitted document, https://www.unfccc.int/sites/default/files/2026-04/INDIA%20NDC%202031-35.pdf . Status: **future targets**, not observed 2026 outcomes.
3. **13 April 2026 — Bureau of Energy Efficiency invited applications for Accredited Carbon Verification agencies.** Illustrates the MRV infrastructure associated with India's carbon market; accreditation invitation alone does not establish completed credit trades. Primary: https://www.beeindia.gov.in/view_content.php?lang=1&lid=569 . Status: **administrative implementation step**.
4. **22 February 2024 — Green Credit tree-plantation methodology notification.** Follows the Green Credit Rules, 2023 and addresses specified tree-plantation activities. Do not equate a green credit with one tonne of CO2 or a verified carbon-market certificate. Primary: https://moef.gov.in/storage/tender/SO-884%28E%29-%5B22-02-2024%5D-Notification-on-Methodology-for-calculation-of-Green-Credit-in-respect-of-Tree-Plantation.pdf . Status: **notified methodology**, subject to later amendments.
5. **2026 — Telangana's industrial pollution-consent workflow.** State government materials distinguish Consent for Establishment and Consent for Operation, and describe processing through TG-iPASS/online systems. Primary: https://sangareddy.telangana.gov.in/pollution-control-board/ and https://ipass.telangana.gov.in/UI/TSiPASS/frmSingleWindowDashboard.aspx . Status: **administrative system**; portal processing counts are not proof of pollutant reductions.
6. **2019 establishment / ongoing — Telangana CAMPA.** State CAMPA was established under the compensatory afforestation framework by G.O.Ms.No.6 dated 15 February 2019. Primary: https://tgcampa.telangana.gov.in/AboutCAMPA.aspx . Status: **institutional history**; stated forest improvements require separate ecological verification.

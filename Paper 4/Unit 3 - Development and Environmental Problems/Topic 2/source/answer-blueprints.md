1. **Forest commercialisation:** define; colonial restrictions; present timber/NTFP value chains; distributional risks; FRA/CFR rights; Telangana forest livelihoods; harvesting and market safeguards; balanced conclusion.
2. **FRA–PESA–forest law:** distinguish purposes and territorial scope; claims institutions; Scheduled Area Gram Sabha; forest diversion and EIA; alternatives; public records; enforceable conditions; conclusion on lawful development.
3. **Groundwater:** hard-rock hydrogeology; recharge and quality; stage-of-extraction formula; May 2026 Telangana depth observation and limitations; pumping incentives; equity; aquifer budget and crop transition.
4. **Surface water:** basin definition; volume versus timing and quality; competing sectors and environmental flows; return flow/rebound; Godavari–Krishna/tank/Musi examples; institutions; monitoring.
5. **Four Fs:** define four uses; crop and water opportunity costs; biofuel and cotton debates; commons and soil degradation; Telangana dryland versus irrigated zones; market and rights safeguards.
6. **Mining:** economic rationale; opencast/underground impacts; MMDR versus EIA/forest/FRA permissions; Singareni; DMF; rehabilitation, mine closure, just transition and accountability.

**For all answers:** use question-specific headings rather than a single block of prose. Approximately 200–250 words per model answer. Do not use invented official question numbers.

## Dated, source-verified developments

- **19 February 2024:** Notification 02/2024 provides the official Paper IV, Unit III, Topic 3 wording. This fixes the chapter scope.
- **25 October 2014 / 14 May 2015:** Telangana Biodiversity Board records its establishment and subsequent state rules. These are institutional dates, not new 2026 developments.
- **December 2022:** The Kunming–Montreal framework was adopted, establishing four long-term goals and 23 targets for 2030. The 30% conservation/restoration numbers are targets, not certified achievements.
- **2019:** IPBES estimated that up to one million animal and plant species are threatened with extinction. The estimate is a risk assessment and should not be misquoted as an extinction tally.
- **24 October 2026 source check:** Telangana Forest Department currently describes 2 tiger reserves, 3 national parks and 7 wildlife sanctuaries. Website-reported counts may change; do not treat them as independent current field censuses.

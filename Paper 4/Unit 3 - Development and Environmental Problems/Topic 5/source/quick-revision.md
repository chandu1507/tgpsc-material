# Quick revision

1. Weather is short-term atmospheric condition; climate is its long-term distribution and variability.
2. Global warming is rising global mean surface temperature; climate change includes wider system shifts.
3. The enhanced greenhouse effect is caused principally by human emissions of long-lived greenhouse gases.
4. IPCC AR6: human activities unequivocally caused warming; 2011–2020 was about 1.1°C above 1850–1900.
5. WMO State of Global Climate 2025, released 23 March 2026: 2025 about 1.43°C above pre-industrial.
6. 2015–2025 were the eleven warmest years in the instrumental record.
7. Annual exceedance of 1.5°C does not alone equal a long-term Paris threshold crossing.
8. Positive feedback example: loss of reflective ice increases absorption and warming.
9. Ocean heat content integrates most of the planetary excess heat.
10. Attribution examines changes in probability and intensity, not simplistic single-event causation.
11. Hazard × exposure × vulnerability, moderated by capacity, shapes disaster risk.
12. Adaptation reduces harm from climatic effects; mitigation reduces causes of warming.
13. Maladaptation example: unlimited borewell extraction during drought depletes future groundwater.
14. Carbon dioxide intensity equals emissions divided by GDP; absolute emissions can rise as intensity falls.
15. Installed non-fossil power capacity is not the same as non-fossil electricity generation.
16. Paris Agreement: well below 2°C, pursue 1.5°C; adopted 2015, entered into force 2016.
17. CBDR-RC acknowledges differentiated responsibilities and capacities.
18. India 2035 NDC: 47% lower GDP emissions intensity than 2005 baseline.
19. India 2035 NDC: about 60% cumulative non-fossil installed electric-power capacity.
20. India 2035 NDC: 3.5–4.0 GtCO2e forest-and-tree-cover carbon sink relative to 2005.
21. India official NDC: non-fossil installed capacity 52.57% as of 28 February 2026.
22. India 2070 net-zero is a future objective, not an achieved result.
23. Telangana EPTRI: climate nodal agency designated through GO Ms No 06, 17 March 2016.
24. Telangana State Action Plan on Climate Change prepared 2015; EPTRI reports revision work.
25. Urban heat island is a local land-cover effect interacting with global warming.
26. Sustainable development balances present needs, future needs, equity and ecological limits.
27. UN 2030 Agenda: 17 SDGs, 169 targets; SDG 13 is climate action.
28. Credible forest carbon claims need additionality, permanence, leakage and verification.
29. Just transition means worker and community protection during structural decarbonisation.
30. Official PYQs require original question paper and question number; all questions here are original.

## Q6 — GVA definition — B
- **A rejected:** Adding intermediate consumption double-counts purchased inputs; MoSPI defines value added after deduction.
- **B supported:** GVA equals value of output **minus** intermediate consumption in the production period.
- **C rejected:** Profit less wages is not the national-accounts identity; labour compensation and operating surplus are components of value added, not its defining subtraction.
- **D rejected:** Tax receipts divided by population is a per-capita fiscal ratio, not production value added.
- **Source:** MoSPI, National Sample Survey Office definitions, undated FAQ: https://mospi.gov.in/press-release/current-surveys-0 . **Confidence:** high for definition; no Telangana-specific data implied.

## Q7 — incremental GVA arithmetic — C
- **A rejected:** ₹40 is the incremental selling price (₹140−₹100), not incremental GVA when intermediate inputs also increase.
- **B rejected:** ₹25 is the *extra intermediate cost*, not net value created.
- **C supported:** (₹140−₹100)−₹25 = **₹15** additional GVA per unit, **conditional on** unchanged other baseline quantities and no other intermediate-input changes.
- **D rejected:** ₹65 adds ₹40 and ₹25, contrary to the GVA identity.
- **Source:** MoSPI GVA definition (same as Q6); numbers are an **invented worked example**, not official statistics. **Confidence:** high conditional arithmetic.

## Q11 — technology readiness levels — B
- **A rejected:** A mature demonstrator may still be uneconomic; NASA TRLs do not guarantee profit.
- **B supported:** NASA explicitly defines TRL as a scale for **technological maturity**, from early principles to operational use.
- **C rejected:** Patent ownership and licensing are separate legal matters, not TRL criteria.
- **D rejected:** Worker productivity is an outcome indicator; TRL measures maturity of a technology.
- **Source:** NASA, *Technology Readiness Levels*, 27 September 2023: https://www.nasa.gov/directorates/somd/space-communications-navigation-program/technology-readiness-levels/ . **Confidence:** high for NASA's definition; sector-specific TRL adaptations may differ.

## Q18 — National Quantum Mission outlay — B
- **A rejected:** ₹60 crore is far below the approved mission outlay.

## Q22 — NISAR soil-moisture data — B
- **A rejected:** **100-metre spatial resolution** describes a gridded data product, not a sensor reading for every plant or a guarantee of plot-level truth.
- **B supported with qualification:** ISRO announced a **100-metre Level-4 satellite-derived soil-moisture product** on **14 February 2026** for irrigation and drought planning. Ground-validation advice is a **methodological inference**, not a claim that ISRO's page measured validation for every field.
- **C rejected:** A remote-sensing product does not eliminate agronomic extension or local observations.
- **D rejected:** Soil-moisture estimates cannot guarantee rainfall.
- **Source:** ISRO, 14 February 2026: https://www.isro.gov.in/NisarData_soil_moisture_products.html . **Confidence:** high for product description; field-level accuracy requires separate validation.

## Q23 — NRSC mandate — B
- **A rejected:** Election administration is not the ISRO NRSC's mandate.

- **C rejected:** Setting policy interest rates is not an NRSC activity.
- **D rejected:** Geospatial/cadastral mapping is **not equivalent** to legal registration or adjudication of every land title.
- **Source:** ISRO, official NRSC profile: https://www.isro.gov.in/ISRO_EN/NRSC.html . **Confidence:** high; land-rights distinction is a legal-methodological caution.

## Standalone quick revision

 — thirty high-value facts

1. **Science** tests explanations; **technology** applies knowledge; **innovation** means useful adoption, not merely invention.
2. **Research → prototype → validation → scale → diffusion** is a pathway, not an automatic sequence.
3. Classical Indian mathematics included place-value traditions, zero and developments in algebra and astronomy; distinguish textual evidence from modern legends.
4. Aryabhata's *Aryabhatiya* dates to 499 CE; do not attribute every later discovery to him.
5. Sushruta and Charaka traditions contain systematic medical knowledge but are not substitutes for modern clinical trials.
6. Wootz steel demonstrates materials know-how; performance depends on controlled process and reproducibility.
7. The **Green Revolution** joined improved seeds with irrigation, fertiliser, extension, markets and policy; it was not a seed-only event.
8. **Total factor productivity** measures output change not explained simply by measured labour and capital inputs.
9. **Value addition** includes reduced defects, energy savings, safer work, faster logistics and better service outcomes.
10. A laboratory result is not the same as a certified, affordable, maintainable field deployment.
11. **TRL** means Technology Readiness Level: a staged indication of technical maturity, not commercial success.
12. **Positive externalities** justify public support for basic research; environmental harms require safeguards.
13. **ANRF** is established under the 2023 Act; the governing board first met in September 2024. [S06]
14. **Vigyan Dhara**, approved August 2024, consolidated three DST umbrellas with an announced ₹10,579.84-crore outlay for 2021–22 to 2025–26. [S07]
15. **RDI Fund:** Cabinet approval 1 July 2025, formal launch 3 November 2025; ₹1 lakh crore is a programme envelope, not money already spent. [S08]
16. DST's latest parliamentary figures put **GERD/GDP at 0.84% in 2023–24**, not a 2026 estimate. [S09]
17. **IndiaAI Mission:** approved outlay ₹10,371.92 crore over five years; an approved budget does not guarantee safe or effective AI. [S10]
18. **National Quantum Mission:** ₹6,003.65 crore for 2023–24 through 2030–31. [S11]
19. **BioE3 Policy** was approved 24 August 2024 to promote high-performance biomanufacturing. [S12]
20. **GenomeIndia** made a dataset of over 10,000 whole-genome sequences available for research in January 2025. [S13]
21. **Semiconductor Mission:** announced ₹76,000-crore programme; chip design, fabrication and assembly/testing are different activities. [S14]
22. **NISAR soil moisture** products described by ISRO in February 2026 use radar observations for agricultural decisions; they do not replace ground validation. [S15]
23. **NRSC** in Hyderabad acquires/processes Earth-observation data and supports mapping and disaster decisions. [S16]
24. **Industry 4.0** combines sensors, connectivity, data analytics, automation and cyber-physical systems.
25. **MSME adoption** is constrained by credit, skills, standards, interoperability and maintenance, not just software access.
26. **Urban digital twins** are models requiring updated data, validation and institutional accountability.
27. **Smart cities** require governance of housing, transport, water, sanitation, inclusion and ecological limits.
28. **T-Works** is a Telangana-supported 78,000-square-foot hardware prototyping centre. [S18]
29. **RICH** connects research and commercialisation; T-Hub, WE Hub and TASK serve different innovation-system functions. [S19]
30. For every technology answer, write **principle → application → Indian/Telangana example → benefits → risks → implementation → conclusion**.

## Answer blueprints — six distinct approaches

1. **Policy evolution:** context → 1958 institutions → 1983 self-reliance → 2003 translation → 2013 innovation → 2020 draft status → critical comparison and conclusion.
2. **Technology missions:** definition → baseline/theory of change → NSM, NM-ICPS, NQM, IndiaAI → output/outcome distinction → inclusion, monitoring and accountability.
3. **ICT and governance:** data-to-information chain → hardware/software/networking → rural applications → cybersecurity and accessibility → reliable service indicators.
4. **Robotics:** sensors and actuators → closed-loop control → public applications → economic and safety constraints → institutional safeguards and human oversight.
5. **Nanotechnology:** scale and property mechanisms → top-down/bottom-up → applications → exposure, lifecycle and scale-up risks → evidence-led regulation.
6. **Communications and digital divide:** fibre/radio/satellite → backhaul versus last mile → affordability and literacy → data protection and resilience → state-level outcomes.

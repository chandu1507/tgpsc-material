# Cycle 138 — Ten original practice MCQs: four-option, source-bounded author review

**Date:** 2026-10-31. **Topic:** 5-1-2. **Status:** AUTHOR-SIDE PARTIAL REVIEW ONLY; NOT independent academic verification; NOT official TGPSC PYQs. Original question stems, answer positions and 30-question bank preserved. Prior Cycle50 key-only checks for Q5/Q8/Q9/Q10/Q11 do **not** constitute previous distractor verification. This document reviews Q1–Q5, Q8–Q11 and Q30, with 10 proposed keys and 30 distractors; 20 keys and 60 distractors remain for future review.

## Q1 — Key A (Scientific Policy Resolution 1958)
- **A, supported:** The Government of India resolution is explicitly dated 4 March 1958 and calls for scientific research, personnel training and a research climate. [P1]
- **B, not the first:** The Technology Policy Statement is dated January 1983, 25 years later. [P2]
- **C, not the first:** STI Policy 2013 is a later milestone in DST's four-policy chronology. [P4]
- **D, not the first:** The STIP 2020 text was released as a consultation draft, much later and without proof of final adoption in this review. [P5]

## Q2 — Key B (technological self-reliance versus autarky)
- **A, rejected:** An absolute import ban is not the meaning of adaptive capability; the 1983 text addresses dependence, indigenous technology and appropriate acquisition. [P2]
- **B, supported interpretation:** The ability to select, adapt and maintain technology is consistent with the Statement's self-reliance objective. This is an analytical paraphrase, not a verbatim statutory definition. [P2]
- **C, rejected:** The 1983 policy is not a monopoly grant to government laboratories; its objective concerns societal technology choices. [P2]
- **D, rejected:** Autarky means avoiding outside trade; that is stronger than the policy's capability-centred approach. [P2]

## Q3 — Key B (January 1983 document)
- **A, rejected:** STI Policy 2013 was formulated decades later. [P4]
- **B, supported:** The original government text carries the heading “Technology Policy Statement, January 1983.” [P2]
- **C, rejected:** The Scientific Policy Resolution is explicitly dated 4 March 1958. [P1]
- **D, rejected:** The National Quantum Mission received Cabinet approval on 19 April 2023; it is a mission, not the 1983 policy. [P8]

## Q4 — Key B (chronological order)
- **A, rejected:** It reverses 1958 and 1983. [P1][P2]
- **B, supported:** DST lists the adopted milestones in the order 1958, 1983, 2003, 2013. [P4]
- **C, rejected:** The 2003 policy cannot precede the 1983 Statement. [P2][P3]
- **D, rejected:** It puts 1958 after 2003 and 2013, contradicting the dated originals. [P1][P3][P4]
- **Scope:** The question says *four historically adopted* policies; it deliberately excludes the **draft** STIP 2020. [P5]

## Q5 — Key B (STIP 2020 status)
- **A, rejected:** A DST policy-consultation document is not a constitutional amendment. [P5]
- **B, supported:** DST expressly labels the fifth STIP text a draft and solicits public comments. [P5]
- **C, rejected:** The consultation text is not a telecom regulation; its described scope is national STI governance. [P5]
- **D, rejected:** The National Quantum Mission was separately approved in 2023. [P8]
- **Qualification:** This review does not establish whether a later formally adopted replacement exists; do not assert final legal status without a subsequent instrument.

## Q8 — Key A (National Supercomputing Mission governance)
- **A, supported:** DST states that DST and MeitY jointly steer NSM, with C-DAC and IISc implementing. [P6]
- **B, rejected:** Election Commission and RBI are not the named steering bodies on DST's mission page. [P6]
- **C, rejected:** ICMR and NHAI are not the listed joint steering bodies. [P6]
- **D, rejected:** Ministry of Tourism and CAG are not the listed joint steering bodies. [P6]
- **Scope:** Steering responsibility does not by itself demonstrate current installed compute capacity or utilisation.

## Q9 — Key A (Telangana NM-ICPS hub)
- **A, supported:** DST identifies IIT Hyderabad's TiHAN as the autonomous navigation and data-acquisition technology innovation hub. [P7]
- **B, rejected:** NITI Aayog's Delhi office is not TiHAN and is not the Telangana hub in the question. [P7]
- **C, rejected:** The Sriharikota launch complex is not the named IIT Hyderabad NM-ICPS hub. [P7]
- **D, rejected:** RBI's central office is unrelated to the identified hub. [P7]
- **Scope:** A testbed/hub is not proof that autonomous vehicles have passed unrestricted road certification.

## Q10 — Key D (NQM approval year)
- **A, rejected:** 1958 dates the Scientific Policy Resolution, not the Quantum Mission. [P1][P8]
- **B, rejected:** 2003 dates the S&T policy, not NQM approval. [P3][P8]
- **C, rejected:** 2013 dates STI Policy, not NQM approval. [P4][P8]
- **D, supported:** DST states Union Cabinet approval on **19 April 2023**. [P8]
- **Scope:** Approval is not evidence of delivery of planned quantum computers or networks.

## Q11 — Key A (₹6,003.65-crore NQM figure)
- **A, supported:** DST gives this as the **approved total mission cost** spanning FY 2023–24 through 2030–31. [P8]
- **B, rejected:** The cited amount is not described as one district's annual expenditure. [P8]
- **C, rejected:** The figure is not a commercial sales total; DST describes a mission outlay. [P8]
- **D, rejected:** It is not described as a 2026 tax rebate. [P8]
- **Scope:** An approved multi-year outlay must not be called fully disbursed or spent.

## Q30 — Key A (constitutional scientific temper)
- **A, supported:** Part IVA, Article 51A(h) directs citizens to develop scientific temper, humanism and the spirit of inquiry and reform. [P9]
- **B, rejected:** Article 110 concerns Money Bills, not the scientific-temper fundamental duty. The **positive** answer is established directly by Article 51A(h); separate clause-by-clause review of Article 110 was not undertaken here. [P9]
- **C, rejected:** Article 280 concerns the Finance Commission, not the named duty; positive correspondence is to 51A(h). [P9]
- **D, rejected:** Article 356 concerns constitutional machinery in states, not the named duty; positive correspondence is to 51A(h). [P9]
- **Scope:** A fundamental duty does not itself confer an enforceable entitlement to an individual research grant.

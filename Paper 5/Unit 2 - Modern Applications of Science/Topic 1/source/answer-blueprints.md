## Answer-writing blueprints for six original mains questions

1. **Crop science and food security:** Define crop science → genetics and management → food availability and stability → pulses, millets and biofortification → climate/resource limits → Telangana and measurable outcomes.
2. **Crops, forests and agroforestry:** Define three land-use systems → products and ecosystem services → competition and trade-offs → community rights and regeneration → Telangana application → differentiated policy.
3. **Medicinal and aromatic plants:** Botanical identity → cultivation and wild collection → distillation and processing → GACP, contaminants and safety → buyers and conservation → pilot recommendation.
4. **Parthenium and harmful plants:** Weed versus invasive distinction → ecological/health impacts → seed-bank biology → safe integrated control → Telangana coordination → monitoring and restoration.
5. **Seed improvement and rights:** Germplasm → selection and multi-location testing → quality seed chain → farmers' contributions → PPV&FR and conservation → equitable access.
6. **Crop diversification:** Diagnose water, soils and markets → test alternative crops → seed and IPM package → procurement/credit/insurance → monitor net returns and aquifers → adapt recommendations.

Each complete model answer uses explicit introduction, analytical subheadings, Telangana/policy application and conclusion. These blueprints supplement, not replace, the complete answers.

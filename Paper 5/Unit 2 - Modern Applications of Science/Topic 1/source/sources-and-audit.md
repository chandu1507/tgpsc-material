### Academic audit — unresolved work

- The syllabus wording is verified against the original commission-hosted notification, but an independent subject-expert syllabus coverage audit is still required.
- ICAR institutional webpages are dynamic; dated releases are preferred for time-sensitive claims. No invented 2026 crop-area, yield, forest-cover or farm-income figures are inserted.
- Crop-specific physiological statements are standard textbook knowledge, but individual cultivar performance requires trial reports for a particular agro-climatic zone.
- The distinction between a medicinal plant and a proven medicine is maintained. No treatment or dosage recommendations are made.
- Telangana case studies distinguish verified institutional activity from hypothetical policy proposals.
- All 30 MCQs and six mains questions are newly authored practice. No official previous-year question is claimed without a commission paper and question number.
- Zero academic approvals are recorded; this draft requires independent factual, botanical-name, source-link and question-difficulty review.

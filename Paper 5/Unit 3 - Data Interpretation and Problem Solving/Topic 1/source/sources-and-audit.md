# Cycle149 — Topic 5-3-1: bounded author-side four-option MCQ audit

**Date:** 1 November 2026. **Scope:** ten existing original practice questions; NOT official TGPSC PYQs and NOT an independent expert review. No original questions or answers are replaced. Earlier Cycle59 internal arithmetic checks for 30 keys/90 distractors remain internal checks, not a substitute for source-correlated academic review.

### Q02 — Percentage points versus relative increase [S17]

**Existing key: C.**

- **A —** A incorrect: 80-64=16 percentage points, not 16% relative.
- **B —** B incorrect: 25% is the relative increase, not a 25-point increase.
- **C —** C correct: 16 points; 16/64 x 100=25% relative to baseline.
- **D —** D incorrect: 80% is the final level, not the change.

**Qualification:** D is a weak distractor.

### Q03 — Nominal district categories and bar charts [S17,S18]

**Existing key: B.**

- **A —** A incorrect: a line implies an ordering between unrelated districts.
- **B —** B correct: a bar chart compares five nominal categories at one time.
- **C —** C incorrect: overlapping reasons cannot be exhaustive non-overlapping pie slices.
- **D —** D incorrect: a time-series curve needs observations across time.

**Qualification:** A correctly designed pie could show district shares, but not the offered overlapping-reasons chart.

### Q07 — Pie angle from a 25% share [S17]

**Existing key: C.**

- **A —** A incorrect: 25% is not 25 degrees.
- **B —** B incorrect: 45 degrees represents 12.5%.
- **C —** C correct: 0.25 x 360 degrees=90 degrees.
- **D —** D incorrect: 120 degrees represents one-third.

**Qualification:** Requires mutually exclusive parts of a single defined total.

### Q10 — Truncated bar-axis baseline [S18,S19]

**Existing key: B.**

- **A —** A incorrect: visual distortion does not necessarily make data values false.
- **B —** B correct: a magnitude bar axis starting at 90 exaggerates visible length differences.
- **C —** C incorrect: axis truncation does not remove time intervals.
- **D —** D incorrect: 360 degrees concerns pie angles, not bar percentages.

**Qualification:** D is implausible and should be strengthened in a controlled future revision.

### Q11 — Missing month in a line graph [S20]

**Existing key: C.**

- **A —** A incorrect: missing is not an observed zero.
- **B —** B incorrect: an unlabelled connecting segment may imply an observed July.
- **C —** C correct: show a gap or clearly labelled justified imputation.
- **D —** D incorrect: removing other valid months destroys useful data.

**Qualification:** ONS advises gaps for missing observations in regular time series.

### Q13 — Multiple-response categories [S17]

**Existing key: B.**

- **A —** A incorrect: a 130% pie does not encode a single partition.
- **B —** B correct: separate bars with a multiple-response note avoid false exclusivity.
- **C —** C incorrect: negative pie slices do not repair overlapping categories.
- **D —** D incorrect: a line between unrelated reasons invents ordering.

**Qualification:** Specify whether denominator is respondents or all responses.

### Q17 — Changing shares and absolute allocations [S17]

**Existing key: C.**

- **A —** A incorrect: old=0.30x100=30 crore, new=0.25x160=40 crore.
- **B —** B incorrect: allocation changed from 30 to 40 crore.
- **C —** C correct: the absolute amount rises by 10 crore despite a smaller share.
- **D —** D incorrect: a 25-crore rise is inconsistent with the supplied totals.

**Qualification:** The two budgets are synthetic teaching values.

### Q26 — App-user survey selection bias [S21]

**Existing key: C.**

- **A —** A incorrect: an app-user poll cannot represent all residents without a sampling design.
- **B —** B incorrect: nonusers were not observed; their satisfaction is unknown.
- **C —** C correct: 90% describes responding app users, not the entire population.
- **D —** D incorrect: descriptive satisfaction cannot establish a causal effect.

**Qualification:** NIST provides general data-analysis caution, not a direct study of this invented poll.

### Q28 — Group-specific denominators [S17]

**Existing key: C.**

- **A —** A incorrect: 90/120=75% for women.
- **B —** B incorrect: 64/80=80% for men.
- **C —** C correct: women 75%, men 80%; these are within-group rates.
- **D —** D incorrect: both numerator and denominator are provided.

**Qualification:** Definitions and periods must match in real-world comparisons.

### Q29 — GDP base year and revisions [S22,S23]

**Existing key: B.**

- **A —** A incorrect: colour does not resolve statistical comparability.
- **B —** B correct: base year, price basis, definitions and revisions determine comparability.
- **C —** C incorrect: footnotes may document vital changes and must not be suppressed.
- **D —** D incorrect: changing to a pie cannot harmonise incompatible time series.

**Qualification:** MoSPI documents the 2022-23 GDP base year and later revision context.

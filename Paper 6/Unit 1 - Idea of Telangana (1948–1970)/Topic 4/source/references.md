# Paper VI Unit I Topic 4 — Source audit

## Source register, verification status and limitations — checked 25 October 2026

The following register identifies what each source actually supports. Dates in the register refer to the source document or event; access verification was performed on 25 October 2026. A source being listed does **not** imply every claim in the chapter is independently verified.

1. **Telangana State Public Service Commission, Scheme and Syllabus for Recruitment to the Posts of Group-I Services (2022 commission-hosted PDF).** [Commission PDF](https://websitenew.tspsc.gov.in/preview/PRESSNOTE/Group-SchemeandSyllabus20220603142210.pdf). Search result displayed the exact Paper VI Unit I Item 4 wording; the commission-hosted PDF was identified as the primary syllabus instrument. **Limitation:** direct independent opening of the later Notification 02/2024 syllabus PDF was not achieved in this run; do not claim 2024 original authentication.
2. **Telangana State Public Service Commission, Group-I Mains Paper VI Question Paper Pattern (2023).** [Commission-hosted pattern PDF](https://websitenew.tspsc.gov.in/preview/UFJFU1NOT1RFL0dyb3VwIDEtIE1haW4gRXhhbS1RdWVzdGlvbiBQYXR0ZXJuMjAyMzAxMTgyMDQ4NTIucGRmr95v17a0y2d8i13v). Supports the three-section Paper VI structure and around-200-word answer guidance. **Limitation:** a question-pattern document does not authenticate any practice question as a previous-year question.
3. **Ministry of Home Affairs, Committee for Consultations on the Situation in Andhra Pradesh (Justice B. N. Srikrishna), Report, 2010/issued 2011.** [Official report landing page](https://www.mha.gov.in/ccsap-justice-retd-b-n-srikrishna-reports); [indexed report PDF](https://www.mha.gov.in/sites/default/files/CCSAP-REPORT-060111.pdf). Indexed section 5.15 recounts employment grievances, the 1969 movement, January protests and variation in casualty estimates. **Limitation:** direct PDF retrieval returned HTTP 404 during this run; quoted search-index extracts were examined, and the report should be re-downloaded from the current official landing-page link during independent review.
4. **Supreme Court of India, *A. V. S. Narasimha Rao and Others v. State of Andhra Pradesh and Another*, 28 March 1969; (1969) 1 SCC 839; AIR 1970 SC 422.** [Reproduced full judgment](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/944080/); [second judgment reproduction](https://www.latestlaws.com/latest-caselaw/1969/march/1969-latest-caselaw-94-sc/). The judgment text establishes the 19 January all-party meeting, 21 January G.O. 36, 1957 Act, 1959 Rules and Article 16(3) holding. **Limitation:** accessible versions are third-party reproductions of a primary judicial decision; a certified Supreme Court PDF was not obtained.
5. **Constitution of India, Article 16, especially clause (3).** [Legislative Department, Government of India](https://legislative.gov.in/constitution-of-india/). Establishes the constitutional setting for equality of opportunity and Parliament's limited residence-law competence. **Limitation:** the historical holding must be read in the context of the constitutional text in force at the relevant date.
6. **Ministry of Home Affairs, Annual Report 1969–1970.** [Government archive index](https://www.mha.gov.in/index.php/archive/annual-reports). Official contemporaneous reporting series relevant to independent chronology checks. **Limitation:** this run verified the archive listing, not the report's specific account of the agitation; do not attribute any casualty number to it without opening the underlying PDF.
7. **Government of Telangana, Bhadadri Kothagudem District, History and Industries.** [Official district page](https://kothagudem.telangana.gov.in/history/). Establishes contemporary district geography and the Singareni/industrial context. **Limitation:** present-day administrative boundaries must not be projected unchanged onto 1969 Khammam district.
8. **Government of Telangana, State History and formation overview.** [Official state portal](https://www.telangana.gov.in/). Confirms the state's 2 June 2014 formation date and current official context. **Limitation:** modern state overview is not a primary source for the 1969 daily protest chronology.
9. **Telangana Today, “Know the background for Kothagudem strike” (2022 retrospective).** [Retrospective article](https://telanganatoday.com/know-the-background-for-kothagudem-strike). Gives local narratives concerning the KTPS employment dispute, worker organisers and Ravindranath. **Limitation:** secondary journalistic retrospective; specific headcounts and fast-duration anecdotes require contemporary corroboration. One paragraph contains an internally inconsistent end-date description, so it is not treated as decisive for the fast's length.
10. **Telangana Today, “The emergence of GO 36 in 1969” (18 April 2022).** [Retrospective article](https://telanganatoday.com/the-emergence-of-go-36-in-1969). Provides local protest chronology and secondary discussion of G.O. 36. **Limitation:** where it differs from the Supreme Court's direct description of the order, the judgment controls the legal facts.

**Evidence hierarchy:** For law, prefer the Constitution, statute and judgment; for government response, prefer contemporaneous orders and inquiries; for local mobilisation, triangulate contemporaneous press and independent histories; for numeric claims, require original registers and definitions. The source register intentionally marks unavailable originals and unverified details rather than silently treating indexed summaries as direct examination.

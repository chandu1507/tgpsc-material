# Dated sources and evidence audit

The entries below distinguish primary law or government publication from retrospective and journalistic accounts. Access checked on **25 October 2026**; where a primary historical dataset is missing, the gap is explicitly recorded. No student-facing page-number references are required.

1. **19 February 2024 — TGPSC Notification No. 02/2024, Group-I Services, Annexure-II, Paper VI, Unit II, Item 4.** Original 50-page commission-hosted PDF opened and wording verified. https://websitenew.tgpsc.gov.in/preview/RElSRUNUUkVDUlVJVE1FTlROT1RJL05PVElGSUNBVElPTl9OT18wMi0yMDI0X0dyb3VwLUlfU2VydmljZXMgKDEpMjAyNDAyMjAxNDA3MzcucGRmr95v17a0y2d8i13v
2. **1991–92 — Union Budget historical archive.** Official Ministry of Finance index confirms availability of 1991–92 documents; this run did not re-extract the entire 1991 speech. https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/hprevious_union_budget.php
3. **2 May 2000 — Andhra Pradesh Scheduled Castes (Rationalisation of Reservations) Act, 2000.** Original statute hosted on India Code; relevant to the historical sub-classification dispute. https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15886/1/act_no_20_of_2000.pdf
4. **1 August 2024 — *State of Punjab v. Davinder Singh*.** Supreme Court-hosted Constitution Bench judgment located; direct full-document retrieval was intermittently unsuccessful, so this chapter does not assert unverified paragraph-level holdings. https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2010/25536/25536_2010_1_1501_54462_Judgement_01-Aug-2024.pdf
5. **14 April 2025 — Telangana Scheduled Castes (Rationalization of Reservations) Rules, 2025, G.O.Ms.No.10.** Government-hosted notification in KNRUHS admissions document; confirms implementing rules and the 2025 Act. https://www.knruhs.telangana.gov.in/uploads/20250716120025KNRUHS%20-%20MBBS%20BDS%20ADMISSIONS%20UNDER%20COMPETENT%20AUTHORITY%20QUOTA%20FOR%20THE%20AY%202025-26%20-%20RELEVANT%20GOs%20ON%20ADMISSIONS%20AND%20SC%20RATIONALIZATION%20OF%20RESERVATIONS%20GO.pdf
6. **2025 — President of India, Padma award citation for Manda Krishna Madiga.** Government-hosted institutional account confirms MRPS emergence in 1994 and the movement's symbolism; retrospective, not contemporaneous minutes. https://presidentofindia.nic.in/sites/default/files/2023-06/padma_2nd_3.pdf
7. **1996 — PESA and Scheduled Area governance.** Ministry of Tribal Affairs legal compendium explains the Fifth Schedule and PESA framework. https://tribal.nic.in/FRA/data/LandandGovernanceunderFifthSchedule.pdf
8. **Government of India, Ministry of Tribal Affairs — Fifth Schedule information.** Official summary of declaration and governance of Scheduled Areas. https://tribal.nic.in/Clm.aspx
9. **25 July 2024 — Telangana Socio-Economic Outlook 2024.** Official state publication, suitable for contemporary comparisons, not a direct 1990s historical series. https://www.telangana.gov.in/news/2024/07/socio-economic-outlook-2024/
10. **25 July 2024 — Telangana Budget 2024–25 presentation.** Government publication documents contemporary district income disparities, agricultural vulnerability and handloom policy. Partisan claims are not treated as neutral adjudicated facts. https://www.telangana.gov.in/news/press-releases/2024/07/deputy-cm-presents-budget-for-the-year-2024-25/
11. **2026 access — Telangana Handlooms and Textiles Department.** Current administrative description of looms, cooperatives and schemes; cannot be backcast into 1990s counts. https://handtex.telangana.gov.in/
12. **2024–25 — KLB Indian Institute of Handloom Technology.** Official institutional history confirms establishment in 2024, relevant to present policy responses, not the 1990s. https://klbiiht.telangana.gov.in/about.html
13. **2 August 2024 — New Indian Express retrospective on MRPS origins.** Secondary historical account of Eedumudi, July 1994 and leadership; contemporaneous founding minutes not obtained. https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/andhra-pradesh/2024/Aug/02/madiga-movement-has-its-roots-in-prakasam-district-andhra
14. **2018–2025 — Tudum Debba public demands.** Later journalistic accounts document current activism, not a verified precise founding date or 1990s organisational register. https://www.thenewsminute.com/telangana/adivasis-declare-self-rule-tribal-dominated-areas-telangana-82295

**Evidence not obtained:** Original 1990s district-wise industrial investment and recruitment datasets on harmonised boundaries; certified farmer-distress and craft-closure historical series; contemporaneous Tudum Debba founding records; complete MRPS founding minutes; district-level budget expenditure reconciliation. Academic approval is withheld until these and other disputed claims are independently reviewed.

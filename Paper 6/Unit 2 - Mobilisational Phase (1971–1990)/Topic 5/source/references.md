3. **1950, as amended — Constitution of India, Article 3.** Primary constitutional authority for state formation. https://legislative.gov.in/constitution-of-india/
4. **1955 — States Reorganisation Commission report.** Original Government of India report relevant to earlier merger debates; use with its historical date and avoid treating it as a report on the 1990s. https://www.mha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2022-08/StateReorganisationCommissionReport1955_0%5B1%5D.pdf
5. **2010 — Committee for Consultations on the Situation in Andhra Pradesh (Srikrishna Committee).** Ministry of Home Affairs official retrospective report, relevant to historical grievance and regional development interpretations; full original PDF was not reopened in this run. https://www.mha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2022-08/CCSAPREPORT_0.pdf
6. **2014 — Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014.** Primary statutory endpoint for the later statehood process, not evidence of inevitability in the 1990s. https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2123
7. **2026 access — Telangana State Government, Language and Culture.** Official institutional description of Telangana's multilingual composite culture and folk forms; contemporary description, not contemporaneous 1990s meeting minutes. https://www.telangana.gov.in/about/language-culture/
8. **2026 access — Telangana Tourism, Culture and History.** Government cultural heritage account useful for institutional recognition of regional traditions. https://tourism.telangana.gov.in/experience/culture-history
9. **University of Hyderabad repository, thesis hosted 2022 — retrospective research on 1997 public meetings.** Academic secondary account describing Bhuvanagiri, Suryapet and Warangal events; original minutes and certified attendance were not retrieved. https://igmlnet.uohyd.ac.in/docs/hi-res/hcu_images/TH12608.pdf
10. **2022 — Telugu-language Telangana movement history, NT News study material.** Secondary retrospective corroboration of the 1997 Bhuvanagiri and Warangal platforms; not an original 1997 register. https://nipuna.ntnews.com/study-material/history/telangana-movement-community-associations-561274.html
11. **2 June 2024 — Telangana State Formation Day official address.** A present-day government articulation of cultural memory; do not use as neutral proof of all historical movement claims. https://www.telangana.gov.in/news/press-releases/2024/06/telangana-state-formation-day-celebrations/
12. **2026 access — Telangana State Portal, state overview.** Official record of 2 June 2014 formation and present-day state institutions. https://www.telangana.gov.in/

**Evidence gaps and limits.** The signed minutes of several 1990s intellectual forums, complete contemporaneous meeting attendance registers, region-wise budget reconciliations, historical appointment rosters, comparable district irrigation delivery series, and a direct line-by-line PDF extraction of the original commission Item 5 clause remain outstanding. Some official historical documents were identified by institutional index but could not be fully opened. None of the original practice questions is labelled an official PYQ. Independent academic review is required before approval.

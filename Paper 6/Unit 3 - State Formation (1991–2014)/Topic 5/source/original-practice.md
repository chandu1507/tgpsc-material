
## Part D — Thirty original explained multiple-choice practice questions

**These are author-created practice questions; no item is represented as an official TGPSC previous-year question.**

### MCQ 1. Which constitutional article directly authorises Parliament to form a new state?

A. Article 3
B. Article 19
C. Article 280
D. Article 356

**Answer: A. Article 3.** Article 3 provides the territorial reorganisation power; Article 4 handles consequential provisions.

### MCQ 2. Under Article 3, the affected state legislature is asked to provide:

A. Binding consent
B. Its views
C. A judicial judgment
D. A national referendum

**Answer: B. Its views.** The President refers the proposal for views; a negative resolution does not veto Parliament.

### MCQ 3. Which constitutional article deals with incidental and consequential provisions of Article 3 laws?

A. Article 14
B. Article 32
C. Article 4
D. Article 368

**Answer: C. Article 4.** Article 4 permits related schedule changes without an Article 368 amendment merely for that reason.

### MCQ 4. The Srikrishna Committee was chaired by:

A. Justice B. N. Srikrishna
B. Justice J. S. Verma
C. Justice K. G. Balakrishnan
D. Justice P. N. Bhagwati

**Answer: A. Justice B. N. Srikrishna.** The Union appointed the committee under retired Supreme Court judge Justice B. N. Srikrishna.

### MCQ 5. Which Srikrishna option kept Andhra Pradesh united with an empowered Telangana Regional Council?

A. Option 2
B. Option 4
C. Option 5
D. Option 6

**Answer: D. Option 6.** Option 6 envisaged continued unity with regional empowerment and safeguards.

### MCQ 6. Which Srikrishna option envisaged separate Telangana with Hyderabad as its capital?

A. Option 1
B. Option 3
C. Option 5
D. Option 6

**Answer: C. Option 5.** Option 5 addressed separate Telangana on existing boundaries with Hyderabad as its capital.

### MCQ 7. The all-party meeting convened by Sushilkumar Shinde took place on:

A. 28 December 2012
B. 9 December 2009
C. 30 July 2013
D. 2 June 2014

**Answer: A. 28 December 2012.** The 28 December 2012 meeting gathered party positions; it was not a binding unanimous accord.

### MCQ 8. The 30 July 2013 Telangana decision was principally a:

A. Presidential notification
B. Congress Working Committee endorsement
C. Supreme Court order
D. Statehood commencement

**Answer: B. Congress Working Committee endorsement.** It was a party-political turning point, not the appointed day or an Act.

### MCQ 9. The Antony Committee in 2013 was primarily:

A. A Supreme Court bench
B. An Election Commission tribunal
C. A Congress political consultation panel
D. A statutory river board

**Answer: C. A Congress political consultation panel.** The Congress panel addressed political and regional concerns; it did not exercise Article 3 powers.

### MCQ 10. Which institution issued the report on Andhra Pradesh alternatives in 2010?

A. Finance Commission
B. Srikrishna Committee
C. NITI Aayog
D. National Human Rights Commission

**Answer: B. Srikrishna Committee.** The Committee for Consultations on the Situation in Andhra Pradesh submitted its report in December 2010.

### MCQ 11. The Andhra Pradesh Assembly adopted an opposing resolution on:

A. 30 January 2014
B. 18 February 2014
C. 1 March 2014
D. 2 June 2014

**Answer: A. 30 January 2014.** The Assembly opposed the bill, but Article 3 did not make its consent mandatory.

### MCQ 12. The Lok Sabha passed the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Bill on:

A. 20 February 2014
B. 18 February 2014
C. 4 March 2014
D. 30 July 2013

**Answer: B. 18 February 2014.** 18 February was the Lok Sabha passage date; the Rajya Sabha followed two days later.

### MCQ 13. The Rajya Sabha passed the bill on:

A. 18 February 2014
B. 1 March 2014
C. 20 February 2014
D. 2 June 2014

**Answer: C. 20 February 2014.** Both Houses had approved the bill by 20 February 2014.

### MCQ 14. Presidential assent to Act No. 6 of 2014 was given on:

A. 1 March 2014
B. 4 March 2014
C. 2 June 2014
D. 30 January 2014

**Answer: A. 1 March 2014.** Assent was on 1 March; 4 March fixed the appointed day through notification.

### MCQ 15. Which notification fixed 2 June 2014 as the appointed day?

A. G.O. 610
B. S.O. 655(E)
C. Article 371-D Order
D. G.O. 36

**Answer: B. S.O. 655(E).** Union Gazette notification S.O. 655(E), dated 4 March 2014, fixed the appointed day.

### MCQ 16. Telangana legally came into existence on:

A. 9 December 2009
B. 30 July 2013
C. 20 February 2014
D. 2 June 2014

**Answer: D. 2 June 2014.** The appointed day was 2 June 2014, not the date of a political announcement or vote.

### MCQ 17. The relevant Act is numbered:

A. Act 6 of 2014
B. Act 29 of 2014
C. Act 55 of 2013
D. Act 3 of 1956

**Answer: A. Act 6 of 2014.** The Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act is Act No. 6 of 2014.

### MCQ 18. Which section provides the temporary common-capital arrangement for Hyderabad?

A. Section 3
B. Section 5
C. Section 8
D. Section 90

**Answer: B. Section 5.** Section 5 provided Hyderabad as common capital for a period not exceeding ten years.

### MCQ 19. Which section specified special Governor responsibilities in the common-capital area?

A. Section 8
B. Section 47
C. Section 90
D. Section 2

**Answer: A. Section 8.** Section 8 addressed certain transitional security and law-and-order responsibilities.

### MCQ 20. The common-capital arrangement made Hyderabad:

A. A permanent Union Territory
B. A separate third state
C. Part of Telangana with transitional common-capital use
D. Part of neither state

**Answer: C. Part of Telangana with transitional common-capital use.** Hyderabad was in Telangana; common-capital use was transitional.

### MCQ 21. The Act-defined population ratio for specified allocations was:

A. 50:50
B. 58.32:41.68
C. 60:40
D. 70:30

**Answer: B. 58.32:41.68.** The ratio was defined for specified purposes, not for every asset regardless of type.

### MCQ 22. Which statement about asset division is accurate?

A. Every asset was split by population
B. Every asset stayed with the old headquarters
C. Different asset categories followed different rules
D. All liabilities disappeared

**Answer: C. Different asset categories followed different rules.** Location, service area and specific statutory provisions could determine allocation.

### MCQ 23. The Act provided for river-management institutions concerning:

A. Narmada and Tapi
B. Ganga and Yamuna
C. Krishna and Godavari
D. Brahmaputra and Teesta

**Answer: C. Krishna and Godavari.** The two inter-state river systems required continuing cooperative management.

### MCQ 24. Which project received special national-project treatment under the Act?

A. Kaleshwaram
B. Polavaram
C. Nagarjuna Sagar alone
D. Mission Bhagiratha

**Answer: B. Polavaram.** Polavaram is addressed specifically in the reorganisation framework.

### MCQ 25. Telangana had how many Assembly constituencies at its 2014 formation?

A. 90
B. 100
C. 119
D. 175

**Answer: C. 119.** The new Assembly had 119 seats; 60 constituted a simple majority.

### MCQ 26. How many Assembly seats did TRS win in the Telangana region in 2014?

A. 42
B. 63
C. 70
D. 89

**Answer: B. 63.** TRS won 63 of 119 seats, sufficient for an independent majority.

### MCQ 27. Who was Telangana’s first Chief Minister?

A. N. Kiran Kumar Reddy
B. N. Chandrababu Naidu
C. K. Chandrashekar Rao
D. E. S. L. Narasimhan

**Answer: C. K. Chandrashekar Rao.** K. Chandrashekar Rao was sworn in on 2 June 2014.

### MCQ 28. Who administered the oath to Telangana’s first Chief Minister?

A. The Chief Justice of India
B. Governor E. S. L. Narasimhan
C. The Lok Sabha Speaker
D. The President of India

**Answer: B. Governor E. S. L. Narasimhan.** Governor E. S. L. Narasimhan administered the oath at Raj Bhavan.

### MCQ 29. Which of these is NOT a direct legal effect of a party committee decision?

A. Party consultation
B. Political reassurance
C. Creation of a new state
D. Gathering stakeholder concerns

**Answer: C. Creation of a new state.** State creation requires the constitutional Article 3 process and parliamentary law.

### MCQ 30. Which statement best describes the relation between legal statehood and state-building?

A. They are identical and instantaneous
B. Legal formation ends all inequality
C. State-building continues after the appointed day
D. Election results determine river rights

**Answer: C. State-building continues after the appointed day.** Legal creation is dated; service delivery, inclusion and inter-state cooperation continue afterward.

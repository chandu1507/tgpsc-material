### Legal and institutional distinctions

| Do not conflate | Correct contrast |
|---|---|
| Srikrishna Committee / Antony Committee | Government-appointed advisory inquiry / Congress party consultation panel |
| Article 3 / Article 368 | State territorial reorganisation / formal constitutional amendment process |
| State legislature's views / consent | Views required on referral; consent not constitutionally mandatory |
| Hyderabad common capital / Union Territory | Temporary common-capital use within Telangana; not a permanent UT |
| Assent / appointed day | Act became law on 1 March; state formed on 2 June |
| Population ratio / every asset | 58.32:41.68 only where relevant statute applies; other categories differ |
| Political victory / statutory commencement | TRS majority did not itself create the state |

### Ten high-yield analytical reminders

1. Name the actor and legal power whenever giving a date.
2. State both Article 3 and Article 4 in a constitutional answer.
3. Treat the Assembly's January 2014 opposition seriously without calling it a veto.
4. Remember that the Srikrishna report listed six options and was advisory.
5. Separate a party's Antony panel from a government committee.
6. Distinguish 18 February Lok Sabha from 20 February Rajya Sabha.
7. Distinguish 1 March assent, 4 March notification and 2 June formation.
8. Explain Hyderabad's temporary common-capital arrangement precisely.
9. Do not apply the population ratio mechanically to every asset.
10. End with implementation, inclusion and cooperative federalism—not celebration alone.

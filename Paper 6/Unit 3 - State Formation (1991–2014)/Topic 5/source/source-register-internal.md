### Explicit unresolved evidence and review limitations

- The original 50-page commission-hosted Notification 02/2024 was not directly reopened in this run; Item 5 wording is corroborated by an independently available complete reproduction. Its typography preserves the source's historical “Anthony” and “Sri Krishna” spellings, although the committee names are commonly written Antony and Srikrishna in historical prose.
- The full original Srikrishna report is listed on the Ministry of Home Affairs portal, but its 11.43 MB PDF was not retrieved for paragraph-by-paragraph verification in this run. The six options were cross-checked using a contemporary published summary.
- The certified 2012 all-party meeting minutes, Antony panel's signed proceedings, original full Assembly debates, and original 18/20 February 2014 parliamentary transcripts were not downloaded. No purported verbatim quotations are presented from those missing documents.
- India Code indexed the authoritative 2014 Act but direct PDF opening timed out. Statutory provisions should be rechecked against the original Act by an independent legal reviewer before academic approval.
- ECI's 2014 statistical-report index was accessible, but the original downloadable election PDF could not be extracted through the JavaScript interface. Seat totals were cross-checked against independent election-result reconstructions.
- Historical political controversies and the comparative success of post-2014 institutions require independent academic audit. All 75 published chapters remain **not academically approved**.

### Final chapter synthesis

The demand for Telangana was resolved legally through Articles 3 and 4 and the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014. The Act established the territory and a transition framework; Parliament's February votes, the President's March assent, and the 2 June appointed day had distinct effects. The TRS majority and first ministry translated movement politics into representative government, but administrative allocation, equity and inter-state cooperation continued beyond statehood.

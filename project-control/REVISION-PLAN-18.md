## Minimum acceptance criteria for each existing topic
1. Verify every official numbered syllabus clause and convert it to a concept-coverage inventory.
2. Every major named concept should have **1–3 substantive pages** where its complexity warrants: historical or conceptual background, causes/mechanisms, named case evidence, law/treaty provisions and consequences where applicable, evaluation, and a Telangana example.
3. Avoid filler and repeated generic points. Explicitly name rulers, monuments, dates, inscriptions and materials; for battles, name the relevant treaties, provisions and consequences.
4. Each mains answer has **question-specific visible headings** (e.g., Introduction, Historical Background, Root Causes, Consequences, Law and Implementation, Way Forward, Conclusion), around the official response length. Include at least 4 specific factual anchors.
5. Preserve a standalone topic summary and quick-revision sheet with memorable distinctions, tables and high-value facts.
6. Keep independent source verification in internal controls; user-facing notes do not require page numbers.
7. Separate original practice from authenticated official TGPSC PYQs. Never invent official paper questions.
8. Render and inspect the PDF, validate the master ZIP, and publish readable chapters + source Markdown to GitHub. Only label PDFs published when their binary GitHub files are verified.
9. Leave academic approval at zero until source-level, legal, factual and editorial review is completed.
